

I’ve tested both ADT and Vivint in my own home. 

If you’re an existing ADT customer looking to change providers or are just starting your search for a new home security system, this article is for you. I’ll be covering three of the top ADT alternatives and give you the reasons why.

## What To Look For in an ADT Competitor

There are 3 main features you want to look for in any alternatives to ADT:

1. Professional security monitoring through a cellular connection
2. Two-way voice through the keypad
3. Remote access to security cameras (view live footage and previously recorded video from an app)
4. Smart home capabilities (control lights, thermostats, door locks, etc)

## Vivint: Best Overall Alternative to ADT

Vivint is a direct competitor to ADT and does everything the ADT system can do, plus a little bit more.

### Monitoring and Panel

Vivint is a full service home security system. It is monitored 24/7 to police, fire and medical. It comes with a touchscreen keypad, just like ADT. It operates over a cellular connection (no need for a landline or Wi-Fi) and has two-way talk built into the panel.

### Cameras and Deter Mode

Vivint offers some features with their cameras that ADT does not. Most notably is a feature called “deter mode”. When someone walks in front of a Vivint camera, it plays a whistling sound and a light around the lens begins to flash. You can also attach a floodlight that will illuminate your yard.

The Vivint cameras will also record without Wi-Fi. (ADT cameras will also record without Wi-Fi, but only for a maximum of one hour).

### Smart Home App and Automations

The Vivint system is compatible with any Z-Wave smart home device, just like ADT. But the app is exceptionally well designed and you can get very granular with the automations you create.

When I recorded my video, Vivint was running a promotion for a free doorbell camera and free installation.

## Cove: Most Affordable ADT Alternative

The Cove home security system is a little more affordable than ADT, without sacrificing any of the core features.

Like ADT and Vivint, Cove has a touchscreen panel with two-way voice built in. You get 24/7 professional monitoring to police, fire and medical. It operates over Wi-Fi with a cellular backup.

### Cameras and their limits

Cove security cameras do everything you would expect: view live footage from the app, get motion alerts, talk to people through the camera, etc. The cameras (at least the outdoor one) will record without Wi-Fi.

Where the Cove system falls short is that it only records in short video clips. ADT and Vivint only record in short clips as well, but you do have the option to upgrade to 24/7 recording.

When I purchased my Cove system I paid $250 upfront (with 70% off and a free indoor camera) and I pay $33 per month for monitoring and camera access. $5 of that $33 per month is a credit you can use for future equipment purchases.

## Honorable Mentions: Ring and SimpliSafe

Ring and SimpliSafe are two other popular DIY home security systems. The reason they didn’t make my list of ADT alternatives is because they do not have two-way voice through the control panel. And the cameras will not record in the event of a Wi-Fi outage.

## Which One Should You Pick?

  * Get Vivint if you want all the features ADT has to offer, plus a few extras
  * Get Cove if you want a more affordable alternative to ADT
