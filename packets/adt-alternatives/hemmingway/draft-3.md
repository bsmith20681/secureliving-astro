

# ADT Alternatives

If you’re an existing ADT customer who wants to change home security providers (or are just shopping around and considering ADT), this guide is for you.

I’ve tested the ADT security system in my own home, as well as all the other major home security companies (SimpliSafe, Ring, Vivint, etc.) and I’m here to share my recommendations.

At a glance:

* If you want everything that ADT has (plus more) from a different provider: **Vivint**
* If you’re tired of paying ADT’s high monthly fees: **Cove**

## What To Keep When You Leave ADT

When you have an ADT security system, there are certain features that you’re used to having. And when you start to look at ADT alternatives for your home, you’ll want to make sure that you’re not compromising on these:

* Professional monitoring over a cellular connection
* Two-way voice communication through the alarm system’s keypad
* Remote access to security cameras and recorded video through the app
* Smart home capabilities (lights, thermostats, locks, etc.)

## Vivint: ADT’s Direct Competitor (And Best Overall Alternative)

Vivint is ADT’s closest competitor. It has all the same features as the ADT alarm system, plus a few extras.

### Professional Monitoring And A Smart Touchscreen Panel

Vivint provides 24/7 professional monitoring to police, fire, and medical. The system communicates over a cellular connection (no landline or Wi-Fi required). It comes with a smart touchscreen hub and has two-way voice communication built right in.

So if a burglar is in your house and the alarm is tripped, you can communicate with the monitoring agent through the alarm system’s panel.

### Security Cameras With ‘Deter Mode’

Vivint security cameras are a big step up from ADT. When someone walks into the frame, the camera will play a loud whistling noise, and a bright LED light (or floodlight) will start to flash.

These cameras also have a big advantage over ADT cameras in that they can record *everything* (and save it to the hub) without an internet connection.

### Smart Home Integration Through The App

The Vivint smart home app is my favorite out of any of the security companies. It’s so well designed and you can get very specific when setting up automations.

### Vivint Vs. ADT Pricing

If you’re leaving ADT because of their customer service or the equipment, Vivint is the company for you.

Their monitoring costs a bit less than ADT ($29.99 per month, compared to ADT’s $42.99), but the equipment is more expensive ($599 minimum, compared to $369 for ADT). You can pay month-to-month if you purchase the equipment upfront.

When I recorded my video, Vivint was running a promotion for a free doorbell camera and free installation.

## Cove Home Security System: The Most Affordable ADT Alternative

The other major alternative to ADT that I recommend is the Cove security system.

### Professional Monitoring And A Touchscreen Panel

Like ADT and Vivint, the Cove system comes with a smart touchscreen panel and two-way voice communication. You get 24/7 professional monitoring to police, fire, and medical. The system uses your Wi-Fi connection, but has a cellular backup in case your internet or power goes out.

### Cove Security Cameras

The Cove security cameras have all the basic features that you’d expect: you can view them live through the app, talk to people at your front door, and they’ll record when they detect motion. They also have a built-in SD card so they can keep recording even if your Wi-Fi goes down.

The only con is that the Cove cameras *only* record when they detect motion. There’s no option to have them record 24/7 (ADT and Vivint both have this as an upgrade option).

### Cove Security System Pricing

Where the Cove system really shines, though, is the low price. You can get the alarm system with professional monitoring for just $23 per month. Add security cameras and it’s $33 per month.

When I purchased my Cove system I paid $250 upfront for a full system (3 cameras, panel, door/window sensors, motion detector, and glass break sensor) and I pay $33 per month for monitoring and camera access.

Of that $33 per month, $5 is put towards a credit on your account that you can use to purchase additional equipment.

So if you’re leaving ADT because of the high monthly fees, Cove is the company for you.

## Why Didn’t Ring Or SimpliSafe Make The List?

Both Ring and SimpliSafe are excellent home security systems (and I have them installed in my own home), but they lack some of the core features that you get with ADT.

Specifically, they don’t have two-way voice communication built into the control panel (like ADT, Vivint, and Cove do). And their security cameras won’t record if you don’t have Wi-Fi.

## Which ADT Alternative Should You Pick?

* If you’re looking for a company that has all the same features as ADT (plus a few extras), get **Vivint**.
* If you want to cut back on your monthly expenses but still get the core alarm system features that you have with ADT, get **Cove**.
