I’ve paid for and installed five different home security systems in my own home (ADT, Vivint, Cove, SimpliSafe and Ring). I’ve lived with each one of those security panels, so I know exactly what you give up with each of these alternatives to ADT.

Two alternatives stand out from the rest. The Vivint system does everything the ADT system does (plus better security cameras and smarter home automation). The Cove system has the core home security features of ADT for just **$23 a month** (no contract). I won’t recommend SimpliSafe or Ring because they both lack a feature I won’t do without (I explain which one below).

## What You Need in a Home Security System to Replace ADT

The ADT home security system does four things well for your home’s safety. Most cheaper security systems lack the first two. Don’t settle for a home security system without all four of these:

1. Cellular monitoring (security panel communicates with monitoring center if power or Wi-Fi goes down)
2. Two-way voice through the security keypad (agent can speak to you through the panel if you can’t get to your phone)
3. Remote access to the security cameras (live view and recorded video from your phone)
4. Smart home control (if you use this with ADT today) – control Z-Wave compatible smart locks, lights, thermostats, etc.

All the security systems in this review include the same basic home security equipment (entry sensors, motion sensors, glass break sensor), and 24/7 professional monitoring for police, fire and medical emergencies.

## Vivint – ADT’s Direct Competitor

Vivint is ADT’s direct competitor in the home security industry. After living with the Vivint system in my home for the past 3 years I’d argue it’s a better system than ADT in most respects.

### Security Monitoring and Two-Way Voice

The Vivint alarm system is monitored 24/7 over a cellular connection (no landline required, and doesn’t rely on your home Wi-Fi). The Vivint touchscreen panel also has two-way talk built in (like the ADT system). You won’t be giving up any security or safety features if you switch from ADT to Vivint.

### “Deter Mode” Security Cameras

When someone walks in front of an ADT camera it sees them and records them. A Vivint camera does that, then whistles at the person, flashes an LED ring (built around the lens) and lights up your entire yard (if you have the floodlight attachment). This is called [Deter Mode](/home-security-systems/what-is-deter-mode-on-vivint/) and I guarantee you a burglar won’t stick around after being whistled at and illuminated on a security camera!

Video quality from the Vivint cameras is excellent. They also keep on recording (to the security panel) when your Wi-Fi goes down, while ADT’s Nest cameras stop recording after about an hour (more about that in my article [does Vivint work without Wi-Fi](/home-security-systems/does-vivint-work-without-wifi/)). Both Vivint and ADT sell an upgrade for 24/7 continuous camera recording (**$7 a month** with Vivint, $10 with ADT).

### Smart Home Automation

The Vivint and ADT security systems both work with any Z-Wave compatible smart lock, light switch or smart thermostat. So on paper these two systems appear to be identical. The difference is in the app.

Say you want the security panel to arm, the lights to turn off and the doors to lock automatically every weeknight at 9 PM. You can do that with both the Vivint and ADT apps, but the flow through the Vivint app is just better (and you get more control over each smart device at each step). I go through that difference in detail (screen by screen) in my [Vivint vs ADT review](/home-security-systems/vivint-vs-adt/).

### Vivint Pricing and Contract

Vivint’s monitoring plans range from **$29.99 to $49.99 per month**. Security equipment starts at about **$599** (with professional installation). If you pay for the equipment upfront the monitoring service is **month to month** (no contract). You can also finance the equipment at 0% interest for 42 or 60 months (you’re committed to Vivint until the equipment is paid off, which is longer than ADT).

## Cove: The Budget-Friendly Alternative to ADT

Cove is the alternative to ADT that I’d point most budget-conscious shoppers towards because it’s the only low-cost security system that didn’t sacrifice the touchscreen panel (with two-way voice) to get the price down.

### Touchscreen Security Panel With Two-Way Voice

Cove’s touchscreen panel has two-way voice built in, allowing the monitoring station to talk to you through the panel (just like the ADT system). The Cove system is monitored over your home Wi-Fi, but there is a cellular backup (and backup battery in the security panel), so you’re not left unprotected when the power or internet goes down. Cove also sells smoke detectors, carbon monoxide sensors and flood sensors, so the monitoring center is on top of “life safety” too.

<figure>
  <Image src={covePanel} alt="Cove touchscreen security panel showing the status of doors and windows" />

</figure>

Cove is a “do it yourself” security system. The sensors stick to your doors and windows with 3M adhesive (and come right back off), so this is the security system I’d suggest to renters. It took me about 30 minutes to install the security sensors. The security cameras took most of an afternoon to get them synced to the app, and I haven’t had any trouble since.

### Security Cameras That Record Without Wi-Fi

The Cove security cameras have all the features you’d expect: live view, two-way talk and motion-activated video clips in the app. But each camera also has an SD card, so they keep on recording when your Wi-Fi goes down. These cameras are made by Eufy, so they continue to work (through the Eufy app) if you ever decide to cancel your subscription to Cove (Vivint’s cameras don’t).

  <Image src={coveCamera} alt="Close up of the Eufy SoloCam S34 (Cove’s outdoor security camera) held in hand" />
  <figcaption>Cove’s outdoor camera (a Eufy SoloCam) has its own SD card</figcaption>

There are two things that annoy me about the Cove security cameras. First, they only record video clips (there’s no 24/7 continuous recording upgrade available no matter how much you pay). Second, there’s no smart home control in the app (can’t control locks, lights or thermostats). If you don’t use either of those with your ADT system today you won’t miss them.

### Cove Security System Pricing

Home security monitoring is **$23 per month** with Cove (**no contract**). You pay **$33 per month** to get access to the security cameras. Of that $33 you get $5 back on your account (as credit to purchase more security equipment), so the “true” cost is more like **$28 a month** (and adding extra cameras doesn’t increase the monthly cost). I paid about **$250 up front** for my security equipment (bought during a 70% off sale and got a free indoor camera). That’s about $20 a month cheaper than ADT ($48.99 per month).

## Why SimpliSafe and Ring Didn’t Make the Cut

SimpliSafe and Ring are both popular “do it yourself” security systems (I tested each one in my home for a full review). But these systems both fail on two counts: no two-way voice communication through the security keypad, and the security cameras stop recording if you don’t have Wi-Fi.

SimpliSafe does have one feature that no other security company offers: “Active Guard.” A live agent watches your outdoor security cameras 24/5 (evening through morning), talks to prowlers through the camera and dispatches the police if needed. If you want that feature then SimpliSafe is the security system for you (but basic security monitoring now starts at $32.99 and the security keypad is push-button). If you already own Ring security cameras and want a security system to go with them then Ring starts at just $20 a month. ## Before You Cancel Your ADT System

Read your contract first! ADT contracts are 12 months (if you paid for the equipment upfront). If you financed the equipment you’re committed to ADT until you pay off the balance. In most cases you’ll save money by waiting until the contract is up (instead of paying to get out of it early).

Your Nest security cameras will continue to work (through the Google Home app) after you cancel ADT. The security sensors you have with ADT won’t work with DIY home security systems like Cove, SimpliSafe or Ring. However, Vivint’s pro installer will often “take over” hardwired security sensors (from other brands). Your Z-Wave compatible smart locks and thermostats will also work with Vivint. The Cove system can’t use any of your existing ADT equipment.
