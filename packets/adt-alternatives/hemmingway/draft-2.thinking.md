## What Do You Need in a Home Security System to Replace ADT?

There are 4 things the ADT home security system does well for your home’s safety (most cheaper security systems lack the first 2). Don’t buy a home security system without all 4 of these:

1. Cellular connection – this allows the security panel to communicate with the monitoring center in the event the power goes out or your Wi-Fi goes down
2. Two-way voice through the security keypad – the ability for the monitoring agent to speak to you through the security panel (important if you can’t get to your phone)
3. Remote access to the security cameras – view your cameras live and review recorded video from your phone
4. Smart home control (if you use this feature with your ADT system) – control Z-Wave compatible smart locks, lights and thermostats

All the security systems reviewed here include the basic home security equipment (door and window sensors, motion sensors and a glass break sensor) and 24/7 professional monitoring for police, fire and medical emergencies.

## Vivint – ADT’s Closest Competitor

Vivint is the closest competitor to ADT in the home security industry. I’ve had the Vivint system in my home for the past 3 years and I’d argue it’s a better system than ADT in most areas.

### Security Monitoring and Two-Way Voice

The Vivint alarm system is monitored 24/7 by way of a cellular connection (no landline required, and it doesn’t rely on your home Wi-Fi). The Vivint touchscreen panel has two-way voice built in, just like the ADT system. So, you don’t sacrifice any security or safety features when switching from ADT to Vivint.

![](/images/vivint-panel-2.jpg)
*Vivint Smart Hub panel with two-way voice built in*

### Cameras With “Deter Mode”

When someone walks in front of an ADT camera it will record that person. A Vivint camera will record that person, whistle at them, flash an LED light (built around the lens), and light up your entire yard (if you buy the floodlight attachment). This is called Deter Mode ([learn more here](/home-security-systems/what-is-deter-mode-on-vivint/)). I guarantee you a burglar won’t linger after being whistled at and illuminated on camera!

![](/images/vivint-outdoor-deter-night-time.png)
*Deter Mode in action at night*

Video quality from the Vivint cameras is outstanding. The cameras continue to record (to the panel) when your Wi-Fi goes down, while ADT’s Nest cameras only record for about an hour without Wi-Fi (read my article [does Vivint work without Wi-Fi](/home-security-systems/does-vivint-work-without-wifi/) for more info). Both Vivint and ADT offer a 24/7 continuous recording upgrade ($7/mo with Vivint, $10/mo with ADT).

### Smart Home Automation

The Vivint and ADT security systems will both integrate with any Z-Wave compatible smart lock, light switch or smart thermostat. So, on paper these 2 systems appear to be identical. The difference is in the app.

Let’s say you want the security panel to arm, the lights to turn off and the doors to lock every weeknight at 9pm. You can do that with both the Vivint and ADT apps, but the Vivint app makes it easier and allows you more control over each smart device at every step. I detail this difference in my [Vivint vs ADT review](/home-security-systems/vivint-vs-adt/).

If you pay for the equipment upfront your monitoring service is month-to-month (no contract).
