# Searcher's Intent: ADT Alternatives

## Who is searching

Someone who probably has ADT already. They want to keep having home security, just not with ADT.

## Why they're leaving ADT

1. **The price is too high.** Talk about more affordable systems and what they cost per month compared to ADT.
2. **They're unhappy with ADT's customer service.** They want the same feature set from a different provider.
3. **They're unhappy with the equipment quality.** They want the same feature set with better equipment from a different provider.

## What the article should do

Address those reasons directly. A reader leaving over price should come away knowing the cheaper option, and a reader leaving over service or equipment should come away knowing which provider gives them what they have with ADT now, without the problem they're leaving.
