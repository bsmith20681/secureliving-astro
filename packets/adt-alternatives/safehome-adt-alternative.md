# ADT Alternative

ADT is one of the [best home security systems](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/) we’ve tested. However, it’s not for everyone. There are more flexible options, including SimpliSafe (DIY, no contract), and more advanced smart security systems, such as Vivint. There are also some that offer better balance, such as DIY Frontpoint systems with comprehensive third-party integration.

But do they match ADT’s protection? Let’s compare how SimpliSafe, Vivint, and Frontpoint outperform ADT and what compromises you’ll have to make if you choose any of our recommended ADT alternatives.

## Best DIY Alternative – SimpliSafe

ADT offers a DIY security system separate from its flagship ADT Pro Install security systems, called ADT Blu. However, ADT Blu is not ADT. It doesn’t offer Nest Cams, and instead rely on more budget-friendly ADT Blu-branded security cameras. That’s why if DIY home security is what you’re looking for, we recommend considering SimpliSafe instead.

**\>> Keep Reading:** [The Best DIY Home Security Systems in 2026](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/best/diy-install/)

![SimpliSafe equipment](https://www.safehome.org/app/uploads/2023/08/SimpliSafe-has-some-new-and-improved-equipment-to-test..png "SimpliSafe equipment")

Like ADT, SimpliSafe has upgraded its equipment with new features we couldn’t wait to test.

With both ADT and SimpliSafe being wireless home security systems, they share a few capabilities. Both systems have apps that allow users to monitor their home security system when out and about and cameras that can be placed in key areas of your home. SimpliSafe and ADT also offer 24-hour support and monitoring.

With that said, there are also some key differences between these two systems that should be noted.

First off: automation. When [we reviewed ADT](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/adt/reviews/), we integrated light dimmers, smart door locks, and Nest smart thermostats. Unfortunately, these are tools you won’t be given access to with SimpliSafe; well, except for the smart lock if you buy one with your SimpliSafe system or have an August smart lock.

However, SimpliSafe is more affordable than ADT. [SimpliSafe packages](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/simplisafe/) start at around $250, while ADT’s customized systems typically start at $599. SimpliSafe costs less for monitoring, too, with plans starting at $22.99 per month. ADT’s plans start at $49.99 per month. Even compared to ADT Blu, SimpliSafe still ends up costing less despite similar retail prices after factoring in their regular sales and monitoring that costs $2 less per month.

**Pro Tip:** You can save even more with SimpliSafe by choosing a refurbished home security system. All equipment is tested to ensure it's operating correctly and comes with a lifetime subscriber warranty when you select a professional monitoring plan.

We’d also say that SimpliSafe is generally more flexible. All monitoring plans don’t come with a contract attached, while with ADT, you’ll usually need a minimum three-year contract. If you need a security system you can work with no matter your budget and flexibility, give our [SimpliSafe review](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/simplisafe/reviews/) a quick read.

## Best Advanced Equipment Alternative – Vivint

Like ADT, Vivint offers high-quality equipment and has a strong reputation for providing premium, in-home security and automation options. Widely considered the “top dogs” of the industry, Vivint and ADT both offer [professional installation](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/best/professional-installation/), indoor and outdoor video, and other advanced features. However, while most similarities between the two are deemed perks, some can also be viewed as pitfalls.

![Vivint Home Security Components](https://www.safehome.org/app/uploads/2020/09/Vivint-Home-Security-Components.jpg "Vivint Home Security Components")

Our Vivint home security system came with more advanced equipment than ADT – but it also cost more.

These home security legends might be the closest match when it comes to the features provided. Nevertheless, differences still exist. Vivint, for instance, primarily uses a dual-path connection (internet and cellular) to send out alerts to the monitoring center. ADT connects to the internet first, and then uses the cellular network as a backup if the primary connections fail.

**\>> Find Out:** [Do Wireless Security Systems Work if the Internet Goes Out?](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/wireless-system-work-power-out/)

When it comes to prices, both companies end up with a similar total cost of ownership. [Vivint's plans](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/vivint/) cost less with rates starting at $24.99 per month for monitoring, while ADT's plans start at $49.99 per month. However, most Vivint systems end up costing at least $39.99 per month for monitoring. Plus, Vivint tends to cost more for equipment. Their outdoor camera, for instance, costs $399. A similar one from ADT costs $179.99. Find out if those prices are worth it in our hands-on [Vivint review](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/vivint/reviews/).

**FYI:** Both ADT and Vivint offer comparable rates and features. Both also require a contract for financed equipment. You can pay for your system upfront, but with Vivint, you’re looking at thousands of dollars. We chose to sign a contract and pay everything off over three years. ADT’s equipment is slightly more affordable.

## Best Home Automation Alternative – Frontpoint

Like SimpliSafe and Vivint, Frontpoint is equally on par with ADT when it comes to reliable, high-quality products and some of the most advanced home security options on the market today. Both are also easy to install due to their wireless nature.

![Frontpoint equipment](https://www.safehome.org/app/uploads/2020/08/Frontpoint-Equipment.jpg "Frontpoint equipment")

We found Frontpoint equipment sturdy, but their bulky size can make them a challenge to install.

While similarities between the two are prevalent, we found plenty of differences. For instance, [ADT’s monitoring plans](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/adt/) are customized to your system, whereas [Frontpoint offers tiered plans](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/frontpoint/) starting at $14.99 per month. However, you’ll need to pay at least $44.99 per month for professional monitoring. That puts them in a similar price bracket, but ADT sweetens the deal with a six-month money-back guarantee compared to Frontpoint’s 30-day money-back guarantee.

Keep in mind that you’ll have a selection of additional options available to you with Frontpoint, from garage door sensors and panic pendants to glass break and flood sensors. The system also supports dozens of Z-Wave third-party devices. We integrated several into our home automation routines via the Frontpoint app. You don’t need to be an expert to set these up – Frontpoint walks you through step-by-step. Simply buying additional pieces of equipment will allow you to enjoy comforts like home automation, remote web and mobile access, and remote and secured video. Have a look at our comprehensive [Frontpoint Security review](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/frontpoint/reviews/).

**FYI:** One feature that stands out with Frontpoint is its geofencing capabilities. We were in a rush and forgot to arm the system after leaving the house. Frontpoint sent us a notification once we were outside the virtual “fence”. Thankfully, we could arm the system remotely via the app instead of going back to do it manually.

## FAQs

-   **Can I switch from ADT to another company?**
    
    Yes, you can. However, you’ll need to wait until you complete your contract unless you or the company you’re switching to is ready to pay a cancelation fee.
    
-   **What happens if I cancel ADT?**
    
    Typically, canceling an ADT contract comes with a hefty cancellation fee. You’ll need to review the terms of your contract for applicable charges. That said, if you don’t have a contract for your ADT system, you can cancel without penalty.
    
-   **Can I use my ADT equipment with another security system?**
    
    Some Google Nest devices may work with other systems or even on their own. However, proprietary ADT equipment doesn't work with third-party systems.
    
-   **Will my ADT devices work without a monitoring plan?**
    
    ADT offers a free self-monitoring plan once your monitoring contract ends. Most contracts last for three years. ADT Blu allows you to avoid the contract, so you can cancel anytime. However, ADT Blu is not compatible with Google Nest Cams.
    
-   **Does ADT take their equipment back?**
    
    If you’re eligible for a money-back guarantee, you’ll need to return your ADT security system. However, if you’re canceling your service without a refund, you can keep the devices you’ve paid for.
