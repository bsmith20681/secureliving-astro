# How Do I Change My Monitoring Plan?

We understand that your monitoring needs may change, that’s why we made it easy for our customers to change their plans accordingly.

_**Looking to compare plans before making changes?**_ _Our_ [**_plan comparison page_** (opens in new tab)](https://support.simplisafe.com/page/monitoring-plans) _can help you see what features are available for what plans at a high level._

## Upgrading Your Monitoring Plan

If you wish to upgrade your monitoring plan, you can do so at any time within the SimpliSafe® Mobile App or [**Online Account** (opens in new tab)](https://webapp.simplisafe.com/#/login).

### SimpliSafe® Mobile App

1.  Open the [**SimpliSafe® Mobile App** (opens in new tab)](http://mobileapp.simplisafe.com/) on your phone or tablet
    
2.  Navigate to the three-bar menu on the top left corner of the screen
    
3.  Select [**Plan & Billing** (opens in new tab)](https://mobileapp.simplisafe.com/billing) from the navigation menu
    
4.  If you have multiple locations, select the location on the top of the screen that you’d like to upgrade your monitoring plan for
    
5.  Under Your Plan, select the new monitoring plan you would like to upgrade to
    
6.  If you have multiple locations and would like to upgrade their monitoring plans, repeat these steps for each additional location
    

### SimpliSafe® Online Account

1.  Log into the [**SimpliSafe® Online Account** (opens in new tab)](https://webapp.simplisafe.com/) on your computer using the browser of your choice
    
2.  Click on Monitoring on the left side of the screen
    
3.  Under the display of your current monitoring plan, click on Upgrade
    
4.  Select the monitoring plan you would like to upgrade to
    
5.  If you have multiple locations and would like to upgrade their monitoring plans, repeat these steps for each additional location
    

## Downgrading Your Monitoring Plan

To downgrade your monitoring plan, give us a call at 1-888-783-8441. Our team of specialists will be able to review current offerings and find a plan that works best for you.

_**Please note**_ that only the [**Account Owner** (opens in new tab)](https://support.simplisafe.com/articles/app-features/additional-user-access-for-simplisafe/67c73badf37dcc5148b94461) can downgrade a monitoring plan. You may need to verify your identity via [**Safe Word** (opens in new tab)](https://support.simplisafe.com/articles/account-billing/how-to-find-or-change-your-safe-word/63447844d9a8b404da76cbf3) before our team can make any changes to your account. This added layer of protection is to ensure that only authorized individuals are making changes to your account, to ensure your safety and security.

## Cancelling Your Monitoring Plan

We’ll be sad to see you go, however, if you find that a monitoring subscription no longer fits your needs you can [**contact us via chat** (opens in new tab)](https://support.simplisafe.com/pages/contact-us) or give us a call at 1-888-783-8441.

Where required, residents of specific cities and states can also initiate cancellation of their monitoring plan by clicking on the Account icon on the Home Screen of [**SimpliSafe.com** (opens in new tab)](https://simplisafe.com/), clicking Security Dashboard, clicking Manage Account and initiating your cancellation request at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this online cancellation is only available during standard hours of operation.

_**Please note**_ that only the [**Account Owner** (opens in new tab)](https://support.simplisafe.com/articles/app-features/additional-user-access-for-simplisafe/67c73badf37dcc5148b94461) can cancel a monitoring plan. You may need to verify your identity via [**Safe Word** (opens in new tab)](https://support.simplisafe.com/articles/account-billing/how-to-find-or-change-your-safe-word/63447844d9a8b404da76cbf3) before our team can make any changes to your account. This added layer of protection is to ensure that only authorized individuals are making changes to your account, to ensure your safety and security.

Once your cancellation has been processed, you will receive a confirmation email to the account owner’s email address on file.
