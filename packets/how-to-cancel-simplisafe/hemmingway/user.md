### Facts from Ben's published SimpliSafe review and Cove vs SimpliSafe comparison

- SimpliSafe is month-to-month. No contract, no cancellation fees. You can cancel whenever you want.
- 60-day trial period: return the equipment within 60 days for a full refund. That only applies to equipment bought directly from SimpliSafe.
- SimpliSafe is one of the few security companies that lets you use the system with no monthly fee at all.
- SimpliSafe plans: No plan (free, $0): arm/disarm from the app and live camera views. Self-Monitoring with Camera Recordings $9.99/mo: 30-day video history, app/text/email alerts, scheduled arming. Standard Monitoring $22.99/mo: 24/7 emergency dispatch, cellular backup, text/app alerts. Core $32.99/mo: adds camera cloud storage, Intruder Intervention, video verification, lifetime warranty. Pro $49.99/mo: adds Active Guard Outdoor Protection 8 PM to 6 AM. Pro Plus $79.99/mo: 24/7 Active Guard.
- On the free plan you won't have professional monitoring, cellular backup, or video recording. You're on your own for contacting the authorities.
- SimpliSafe cameras are proprietary. You can't move them to another app or another company's service. Without a paid SimpliSafe plan you lose video recording.
- Ben's opinion: SimpliSafe "isn't the budget king it used to be." Monitoring used to cost around $15 to $20 per month.
- Ben's verdict in the comparison: "For my money, I lean towards Cove. Lower upfront cost, non-proprietary cameras, better keypad, and the same monthly price for core monitoring. But if Active Guard is a must-have or you want the free monitoring tier, SimpliSafe is the move."
- Cove requires a paid monitoring plan. There's no self-monitoring option. Cove's cameras are made by Eufy: if you cancel Cove, you can download the Eufy app and keep using the cameras for free.
- Ben's SimpliSafe system cost $965.89 at list; he paid $519.10 with a 50% off deal. His Cove system cost $250.48 with 70% off and a free indoor camera, including 3 cameras, panel, sensors, a motion sensor, and a glass break sensor.

## Can I cancel SimpliSafe online?

No, you have to call to cancel by calling customer service at 1-888-783-8441. You can upgrade your SimpliSafe account online, but you can't downgrade or cancel it online.

They may also try to offer you a deal. Just say you’re not interested. In our experience, SimpliSafe isn’t pushy.

## Can I cancel SimpliSafe through the app?

No, you can’t cancel through the [SimpliSafe app](https://www.safewise.com/home-security-systems/simplisafe/app/). There’s also no online cancellation option.

## Can I cancel SimpliSafe without a fee?

Yes, you can cancel SimpliSafe without paying a fee. SimpliSafe doesn’t charge any [cancellation fees](https://www.safewise.com/home-security-systems/cancellation/) since there's no long-term contract, and you pay for your equipment upfront. 

## Do I need to return my SimpliSafe equipment after canceling my monitoring plan?

No, the SimpliSafe security system is yours to keep. 

In fact, [SimpliSafe equipment](https://www.safewise.com/home-security-systems/simplisafe/equipment/) works as a basic home alarm without any monitoring service. If someone breaks in while the system is armed, the base station siren will sound, which might be enough to scare them off and alert the neighbors. 

That’s a big "might," though, which is why we recommend [professional monitoring](https://www.safewise.com/home-security-systems/faq/alarm-monitoring/) whenever possible.

## Can I restart my SimpliSafe plan at a later date?

Yes, SimpliSafe lets you start and stop services at any time. If you want to restart or upgrade, you can do so through the SimpliSafe home security app. Call customer service if you want to pause or downgrade.

## Can I cancel SimpliSafe and give my equipment to someone else?

Yes, you can transfer your SimpliSafe system. First, call SimpliSafe to cancel. Then, the new equipment owner can create a SimpliSafe account and activate a monitoring plan with that equipment.
