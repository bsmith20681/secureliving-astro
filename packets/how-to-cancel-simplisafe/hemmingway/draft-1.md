

# How to Cancel SimpliSafe: Step-By-Step Guide

SimpliSafe is a month-to-month service. There is no contract and no cancellation fees. You can cancel whenever you want to by either calling or chatting with SimpliSafe.

## How To Cancel SimpliSafe Step By Step

1. Find your SimpliSafe safe word
2. Make sure you are the “account owner” (or have the account owner cancel the service)
3. Call customer service at 1-888-783-8441 (or you can chat with them online)
4. Tell the customer service rep that you want to cancel your monitoring plan
5. Wait for the email from SimpliSafe confirming your cancellation
6. Check your billing to make sure you’ve been canceled

## Can You Cancel SimpliSafe Online Or In The App?

You can upgrade your SimpliSafe monitoring plan at any time in the SimpliSafe app or in your online account.

But if you want to downgrade your monitoring plan or cancel your service you’ll have to call or chat with SimpliSafe.

If you’re a resident of certain cities and states you can initiate a cancellation request online. You just have to log in to your online account at simplisafe.com (only during standard hours of operation), click on the “Account” icon on the home screen, then “Security Dashboard”, and then “Manage Account.” At the bottom of the screen there is a link to “Initiate Cancellation Request.”

## SimpliSafe Cancellation Fees And Contract

SimpliSafe’s service is month-to-month. You can cancel at any time, for any reason with no penalty.

If you purchase your equipment directly from SimpliSafe there is a 60 day trial period. You can return the equipment within 60 days for a full refund.

## What Still Works After You Cancel SimpliSafe

SimpliSafe is one of the few home security companies out there that allows you to use their system with no monthly fee at all.

If you don’t have a paid monitoring plan you can still arm and disarm your system in the SimpliSafe app and you can view your cameras live.

But you won’t have access to professional monitoring, cellular backup or video recording. You are on your own to contact the authorities if there is a problem with your home.

Also, SimpliSafe “isn’t the budget king it used to be” (in my words). When I first got my system the monitoring used to cost about $15 to $20 per month. Now the cheapest monitoring plan (without cameras) is $22.99 per month.

You can always downgrade to a cheaper SimpliSafe plan if you don’t want to cancel your service entirely.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does SimpliSafe require a subscription?

No. You can use your SimpliSafe system for free. You’ll be able to arm and disarm your system in the app and view your cameras live. But you won’t get any video recording or professional monitoring.

### Can I cancel SimpliSafe online or through the SimpliSafe app?

For the most part the answer is no. You’ll need to call or chat with customer service to cancel your service. But if you live in certain cities and states you can submit a cancellation request online.

### Is there a cancellation fee?

No. SimpliSafe is a month-to-month service so there is no cancellation fee.

### Do I have to return my SimpliSafe equipment when I cancel?

No. However, if you purchased your equipment directly from SimpliSafe and you are within the 60 day trial period you can return the equipment for a full refund.
