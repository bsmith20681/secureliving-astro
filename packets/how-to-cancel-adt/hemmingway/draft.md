

You already have an ADT home security system and you’ve decided you want to cancel. This article will tell you exactly how to cancel ADT, whether you can avoid the ‘retention’ department, how much it will cost you to cancel, and what you can do to minimize the cost.

The short answer is: To cancel ADT, call 800.238.2727, give at least 30 days advance notice and (assuming you’re still in your initial contract term) expect to pay 75% of the remaining monthly service charges. There are, however, a few legitimate ways to avoid that termination fee, which I’ll explain below.

## How To Cancel ADT Service, Step-By-Step

Here are the basic steps for how to cancel ADT service:

1. Call ADT at 800.238.2727 (1-800-ADT-ASAP)
2. Tell the customer service representative you want to cancel
3. Get through the ‘retention’ department (details below)
4. Verify your account
5. Ask about any termination charges
6. Ask for confirmation of your cancellation in writing

Most cancellations can be completed in 10-15 minutes by phone.

### What To Have Ready Before You Call ADT

Have the following details ready before you call ADT:

* Your ADT account details. This includes your account number, the name the account is registered in, your home address and phone number.
* Your ADT contract. You need to know what the term of your contract is (12 months, 36 months, etc.) and the date you signed the contract. You should then be able to work out how many months you have remaining.
* Your own calculation of what the termination charge will be (based on the formula below). It’s worth doing this yourself so you can compare it to the figure ADT gives you.

### What To Expect On The Cancellation Call

When you call to cancel ADT, you’ll be transferred to a special ‘retention’ department whose entire reason for existing is to talk you out of canceling. Expect a range of offers and discounts designed to change your mind.

Eventually, you’ll get through to a regular customer service rep. You’ll be asked some questions to verify you are a genuine ADT customer. You can then ask about the charge for canceling your contract (if applicable).

I’d recommend asking ADT to confirm the details of your cancellation in writing (email is fine). This should include the date your ADT contract will end and whether you’ll be charged an early termination fee.

You need to give a minimum of 30 days notice when you cancel ADT. However, your contract will end on the last day of your current contract term. So, if you cancel 5 days into your monthly contract term, you’ll get 25 days of security service for your last month’s payment.

### Follow Up With Written Notice

According to paragraph 4 of page 5 of my ADT contract, if I fail to give ADT the required written termination notice when I cancel, ADT can withhold 30 days of service charges from any refund it otherwise owes me. That’s not a huge amount of money, but I’ll make sure I send the required paperwork when I cancel my ADT system.

## Can You Cancel ADT Online Or Without Calling?

According to my ADT contract, the only way to cancel is to call a phone number. There is no mention of an online cancellation process. The contract does refer to giving ‘written notice’ of termination, which you could presumably email or mail in. But I wouldn’t count on being able to avoid the phone call.

## How Long Are ADT Contracts?

The length of your ADT contract will vary depending on what version of the contract you signed and what state you live in. My ADT contract has an initial term of 12 months, but many ADT contracts are for 36 months.

When I signed my ADT contract, it cost me $47.99 a month for 12 months (12 payments of $47.99, $575.88 in total). You can find the length of your own ADT contract on page 1 in the section labeled “Your Service Contract Term”.

### Does The ADT Contract Automatically Renew?

According to my ADT contract, the initial 12 month term “renews month to month thereafter.” Specifically, it renews automatically in 30-day terms unless either you or ADT give at least 30 days notice before the end of the current term.

## ADT Cancellation Fee and Early Termination Fee

If you cancel your ADT security system during the initial term of your contract, you’ll be charged 75% of the total remaining monthly service charges. This amount is “payable immediately in full.”

ADT refers to this as a “contract termination charge” and explicitly states that it “is not a penalty.” If ADT cancels your contract during the initial term because you’ve violated the terms of the contract (for example, by failing to pay your monthly bill), you’ll be hit with the same charge.

### How The ADT Termination Charge Is Calculated

Let’s say you signed up for a 12-month ADT contract (like me) and you want to cancel when you have 6 months left to run. You’ll be charged 0.75 x 6 x monthly service charge. My monthly service charge is $47.99, so I’d be charged 0.75 x 6 x $47.99 = $215.96.

Here is the termination charge you would pay (based on a $47.99 per month service charge) at various points during a 12-month ADT contract:

| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| 11                      | $396.92                                       |

| 6                       | $215.96                                       |
| 3                       | $107.98                                       |
| 1                       | $35.99                                        |
| 0 (month-to-month)      | $0                                            |

If you paid a security deposit when you first set up your ADT account, any remaining deposit will be refunded within 45 days after your service ends (less any amounts you owe ADT, including any termination charges). Note that ADT doesn’t refund any credits of $5 or less.

## How To Cancel ADT Without Penalty

There are 4 legitimate ways to get out of your ADT contract without paying the 75% termination charge:

1. Wait Until You’re ‘Month-To-Month’: No termination fee is charged if you cancel during the 30-day renewal periods that follow the initial contract term.
2. Military Relocation: The termination charge is waived for active military personnel who can provide proof of military service and orders to relocate from the home where the ADT system is installed.
3. Object To A Price Increase: After the first year of your contract, ADT can increase your monthly service charge. If you object to the increase within 30 days of ADT sending you notice, and ADT refuses to waive the increase, you can terminate your contract effective 30 days after ADT receives your notice. No termination fee is charged.
4. When ADT Cancels For Its Own Reasons: If ADT has to cancel your contract for reasons such as its monitoring center being damaged, or it can no longer maintain the signal connection between your home and the monitoring center, ADT will refund any advance payments and no termination fee is charged.

### Don’t Just Stop Paying!

If you try to get out of your ADT contract by simply stopping paying your monthly service charge, ADT can (after giving you 10 days notice to ‘cure’ the problem) end your contract and charge the 75% termination fee. But ADT can go further: It can demand the entire remaining balance of your contract (not just 75%) in one lump sum, plus any costs it incurs trying to collect the money (e.g. legal fees), plus interest. Finally, ADT can report you to the credit bureaus. Don’t stop paying!

## ADT’s 3 Day Right To Cancel

Within 3 business days of signing your ADT contract, you have the right to cancel “without any penalty or obligation.” I signed my ADT contract on 1/28/2026 and had until midnight on 1/31/2026 to cancel. 1/31/2026 was a Saturday, which means Saturdays count as a ‘business day’!

If you live in Alaska you have 5 business days to cancel. New York state residents have 7 business days to cancel a ‘personal emergency response unit’ and Alabama residents have 30 business days to cancel an alarm or locking system (if you are 75 years or older).

To cancel during this ‘right to cancel’ period, either call 800.327.4348 (a different number to ADT’s main customer service line) or mail (or deliver) the provided Cancellation Form (or any other written notice of cancellation) to the ADT sales office (address is printed on the form – in my case an office in Aurora, CO, attention “Sales Manager”).

Once ADT receives your cancellation notice, it will refund any payments you made within 10 business days. You need to make your ADT equipment available at your home (in “substantially as good condition as when received”) or, if ADT requests, you need to ship the equipment back to ADT (at ADT’s expense). If ADT fails to pick up the equipment within 20 days of receiving your cancellation notice, you can keep the equipment or get rid of it – you have no further obligation to ADT.

## What To Do After You Cancel ADT

Unless you have some other written agreement, all of the equipment ADT installs in your home is owned by ADT. After your ADT contract ends, ADT can remove, disable or even “abandon” its equipment in your home. ADT may let you keep the equipment if you make an additional payment (amount determined by ADT at the time). I have a whole article about how to use ADT equipment after you cancel.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the ADT Cancellation Number?

The ADT cancellation number is 800.238.2727.

### Can You Cancel ADT Anytime?

Yes, you can cancel ADT at any time. If you are still within the initial term of your ADT contract, you may have to pay a termination fee.

### Does The ADT Contract Automatically Renew?

Yes, after the initial term of your ADT contract expires it automatically renews on a month-to-month basis.

### Does ADT Have A 3 Year Contract?

Many ADT customers are locked into a 36-month (3-year) contract.

### Can I Cancel ADT If I’m Moving?

Yes, you can cancel ADT if you are moving house. You may have to pay a termination fee if you are still in the initial term of your contract.

### Do I Have To Return The ADT Equipment When I Cancel?

The ADT equipment in your home is owned by ADT. After you cancel, ADT can come and remove the equipment, or it can just ‘abandon’ it in your home. You may be able to keep the equipment if you are willing to make a payment to ADT.

### How Do I Cancel ADT Within 3 Days Of Signing?

Within 3 business days of signing your ADT contract, you can cancel without penalty. To do this, either call 800.327.4348 or mail or deliver the provided Cancellation Form (or any other written notice of cancellation) to the ADT sales office (address printed on the form).
