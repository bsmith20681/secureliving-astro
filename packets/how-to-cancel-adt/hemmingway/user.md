#### My contract's basics

- 12 month initial term at $47.99 a month: 12 payments, $575.88 in total (page 1, Order Details; page 3, Financial Disclosure Statement).
- "The initial term of this contract is one (1) year and renews month to month thereafter" (page 2).
- After the initial term it renews automatically in 30 day terms unless either side gives at least 30 days' notice before the end of the current term. If terminated, the contract ends on the last day of the current term (page 5, paragraph 1A).
- The order page says I'll be charged monthly "until you cancel with at least 30 days' advance notice to ADT" (page 1).
- Prepaying the rest of the term early: "there is no penalty or refund" (page 3).

#### ADT's 3 day right to cancel (pages 10 and 11, Notice of Cancellation)

- I can cancel "without any penalty or obligation" within 3 business days of signing. Signed 1/28/2026, deadline midnight 1/31/2026. That was a Saturday, so Saturdays count as business days. The right is also mentioned on page 2 under "Notice of Cancellation".
- Longer windows: Alaska 5 business days; New York 7 business days for a personal emergency response unit; Alabama 30 business days for an alarm or locking system sold to someone 75 or older.
- To cancel in the window: call 800.327.4348 (a different number from ADT's main line), or mail or deliver the cancellation form or any written notice to the ADT sales office printed on the form (mine was in Aurora, CO, attention Sales Manager).
- Payments are refunded within 10 business days after ADT receives the notice.
- Equipment: make it available at the home in substantially as good condition as when received, or ship it back at ADT's expense if they ask. If ADT doesn't pick it up within 20 days of the notice, you can keep it or get rid of it with no further obligation.

#### How to cancel after the 3 days

- "I may terminate my service by calling ADT at 800.238.2727 (1-800-ADT-ASAP)" (page 5, paragraph 2). The contract gives a phone number as the way to cancel. It doesn't mention any online way.
- If I don't give the required written termination notice, ADT can keep 30 days of service charges out of any refund it owes me (page 5, paragraph 4).

#### Cancellation fee and early termination fee (page 5, paragraph 2)

- Canceling during the initial term costs 75% of the total remaining monthly charges, "payable immediately in full."
- ADT calls it a "contract termination charge" and says it "is not a penalty."
- Example from my contract: canceling with 6 months left = 0.75 x 6 x $47.99 = $215.96.
- The same charge applies if ADT cancels during the initial term because I broke the contract, for example by not paying (page 5, paragraph 2; page 8, paragraph 20B).

#### How to cancel ADT without penalty

1. Wait for month to month. No contract termination charge applies during the 30 day renewal periods after the initial term (page 5, paragraph 2).
2. Military relocation. The charge is waived for active military personnel with proof of service and orders to relocate from the home (page 5, paragraph 2).
3. A price increase. ADT can raise the service charge after the first year. If I object within 30 days of the notice and ADT doesn't waive the increase, I can terminate, effective 30 days after ADT gets my notice, with no termination charge (page 5, paragraph 3).
4. ADT cancels for its own reasons. If ADT ends the contract because, for example, its monitoring center is damaged or it can't keep the signal connection, it refunds advance payments and charges no termination fee (page 8, paragraph 20A).

What not to do: just stop paying. ADT can then end the contract and charge the termination fee after a 10 day notice to fix it (page 8, paragraph 20B), demand the entire remaining balance at once plus collection costs and interest, and report me to credit bureaus (page 8, paragraph 16).

#### Other details

- Equipment installed by ADT is owned by ADT unless agreed otherwise in writing (page 2; page 6, paragraph 8). After the contract ends, ADT can remove, disable or abandon it, and may let me keep it for an extra payment ADT decides at the time (page 6, paragraph 8). - Any security deposit is refunded within 45 days after service ends, minus anything owed, including termination charges (page 5, paragraph 1B). ADT doesn't refund credits of $5 or less (page 5, paragraph 4).

## What is the ADT cancelation policy?

Because every ADT security system comes with a required contract, it's important to understand the terms:

-   In order to cancel your ADT contract, you must call ADT customer service at [**800-238-2727**](tel:800-238-2727/).
-   Security system customers **pay up to 75%** of the remaining monthly charges when they cancel early (see breakdown in the table above).
-   ADT's professional monitoring service has a **36-month contract** (24 months in California).
-   There’s a six-month **[money-back service guarantee](https://www.safewise.com/home-security-systems/adt/disclaimer/)** on ADT security systems. The guarantee applies to **system-related issues that ADT hasn't been able to resolve** within the first six months of your contract (see more details below).

### What happens when I cancel my ADT contract early?

When you cancel your ADT contract early, you’ll **pay the applicable early termination fees** (up to 75% of remaining monthly charges), and ADT will send you a prepaid box with **instructions on how to return your ADT equipment**.

### What's included in an ADT contract?

ADT's contract covers your equipment costs, warranty, and professional monitoring. Unlike DIY security systems, which require upfront equipment costs, ADT **includes the equipment cost in your monthly fee**. This can make it easier to get a security system because you don't have to come up with a big chunk of change to pay for the equipment. The contract **doesn't cover installation**, though, so you'll still need to pay at least $99 to have your system installed.

### About ADT's six-month service guarantee

ADT’s money-back guarantee looks like a pretty good deal. Still, it only applies to system issues that ADT has tried to resolve, but couldn't. It doesn't apply to customers who want to cancel out of dissatisfaction or for reasons other than technical problems:

"Money back service guarantee **only applies after ADT has made attempts to resolve a system related issue and has not been able to resolve that issue** within the first 6 months of your contract. Equipment must be fully removed before a refund will be processed. Conditions preventing normal system operation cannot be caused by the customer."[1](https://www.safewise.com/home-security-systems/adt/cancellation/#Sources)

Since ADT’s [terms and conditions](https://www.getsafestreets.com/terms-and-conditions) don’t refer to any other type of return policy other than a “system related issue,” we don’t advise getting an ADT system if you’re hoping to try it out for six months thinking you can cancel for a full refund.

## How to cancel ADT FAQ

It depends on whether or not you bought your ADT system online.

[According to ADT](https://help.adt.com/s/article/How-Do-I-Cancel-My-ADT-Monitoring), "If you purchased your ADT system online, you can cancel your monitoring online." 

**Here's how:**

-   If you use MyADT, log into your account on myadt.com:
    -   Click Account > Manage Subscriptions
    -   Fill out the cancellation form.
-   If you use ADT+:
    -   Log into your account on plus.adt.com
    -   Click Billing > under Manage Subscriptions
    -   Click Cancel Subscription and fill out the cancellation form.
-   If you do not see a cancellation form, you did not purchase your system online. Please call 1-800-ADT-ASAP.

For everyone else, you can't cancel ADT service online. ADT cancelation requires a customer service phone call to [**800-238-2727**](tel:800-238-2727/). During the call, the customer service agent gives you instructions and may ask you to submit a written request. This call is the best time to ask if you’re eligible for a refund or a break on the 75% termination fee.

After your ADT contract ends, it automatically renews on a month-to-month basis until you cancel. There are no termination fees when you cancel a monthly contract after fulfilling the terms of your original 36-month contract. In some cases, your contract might renew for a longer term, so you should always check your contract's terms and conditions for exact renewal details.

See how ADT's policies compare to other [top home security companies' cancelation policies](https://www.safewise.com/home-security-systems/cancellation/).

The early termination fee is 75% percent of the remaining contract. This means if you still have six months left in your contract, you must pay 75% percent of the last six months.

Yes, you can cancel your ADT security system if you move, but you'll still be required to pay the cancelation fees.

If your system wasn't professionally installed, you may be able to take it with you to your new home. However, most ADT systems are typically tied in the contract to the location of your home. This means you would need to contact ADT to transfer the services to your new home.

If you decide to end your contract with ADT early, then you are required to pay up to 75% of the remaining monthly charges. This means if you have a 36-month professional contract and you want to cancel it at 34 months, you must pay up to 75% of the remaining two months.

To cancel your plan call [800-238-2727](tel:800-238-2727). When you cancel, ADT will send you a prepaid box in the mail with instructions on how to return your equipment. However, if you've had issues with your security system and ADT has tried to resolve the problem unsuccessfully within the first six months of your contract, you may be eligible for the [money-back service guarantee](https://www.safewise.com/home-security-systems/adt/disclaimer/).

If you're looking to avoid early cancelation fees and penalties, downgrading to a cheaper plan may save you money. While you might lose a few features included with your security system, it could be cheaper to downgrade rather than paying 75% percent of your remaining balance.
