# How to Cancel ADT Under Contract Without Paying a Penalty

Already got an ADT security system and want to cancel it? You’re in the right place.

If you want to cancel ADT, you’ll have to call 800.238.2727, provide 30 days’ advance notice, and (assuming you’re still within your initial contract term) you’ll be charged 75% of the remaining monthly service charges. There are some legitimate ways around that termination fee that I’ll point out below.

## How To Cancel ADT Service, Step By Step

If you just want to get to the point of this article and cancel your ADT system right now, here are the basic steps to follow:

1. Call ADT at 800.238.2727.
2. Tell them you want to cancel your ADT service.
3. Get through the ‘retention’ department (more on that below).
4. Find out what (if any) contract termination fee you’ll have to pay.
5. Ask ADT to confirm the details of your cancellation in writing.

You can find out more about what to expect during that call below.

### What To Have Ready Before You Call

You should have the following information ready before calling to cancel ADT:

* Your ADT account details (account number, the name the account is registered to, your home address and phone number).
* Your signed ADT contract. This will tell you what your contract term is (12 months, 36 months or whatever) and the date you signed the contract. From this, you should be able to calculate how many months you’ve got remaining.
* Your calculation of the contract termination fee (based on the formula we’ll explain below). Have this number ready so you can compare it to what ADT tells you.

### What To Expect When You Call ADT

When you call to cancel ADT, you’ll be passed to a ‘retention’ department whose job it is to try to stop you from cancelling. They will make a range of offers to try and convince you to stay.

Once you’ve made it through the retention process, you’ll be verified (they’ll ask for details of your account to confirm you’re a genuine ADT customer), and can then ask about the charge for cancelling your contract.

You should ask ADT to confirm the details of your cancellation in writing (email is fine). This will include the date your contract ends and whether you’re charged an early termination fee.

Note that even though you must give a minimum of 30 days’ notice when cancelling, your contract will end at the end of the current billing period. So if you cancel 5 days into your monthly billing period, you’ll still get 25 days of security service for your money.

### Follow Up With Written Notice

According to my ADT contract, if I don’t provide the required written termination notice when I cancel my contract, then ADT can withhold 30 days of service charges from any refund it owes me. That’s not a lot of money but I’ll be sure to send in the required paperwork when I cancel my ADT service.

## Can You Cancel ADT Online Or Without Calling?

My ADT contract says that I have to call a phone number to cancel my security system. It doesn’t mention any other method of cancellation. You may be able to get away with emailing or mailing your request for written notice of termination, but I would not count on it.

In my experience of home security system contracts, ADT’s terms are pretty normal. It’s extremely difficult to cancel any home security company without speaking to a human being.

## How Long Are ADT Contracts?

ADT used to sign all of its customers up to a 36-month contract. That’s no longer the case, but the length of your contract will depend on what kind of deal you get when you first sign up.

For example, my own ADT contract (which I discuss in detail in my ADT review) has a 12-month term. In fact, it states: “The initial term of this contract is one (1) year and renews month to month thereafter”.

### Does The ADT Contract Automatically Renew?

Once you’re through your initial contract term, you’ll be on a month-to-month contract that automatically renews every 30 days unless you or ADT give at least 30 days’ notice before the end of the current term.

If you cancel during this month-to-month period (or ADT cancels your contract), then your contract ends at the last day of the current term.

The optional Quality Service Plan that ADT offers also renews in 30-day terms. You have to give 30 days’ written notice to end it.

You’re allowed to prepay the rest of your initial contract term if you want. According to my contract: “If you prepay the remainder of your initial contract term early there is no penalty or refund.”

## ADT Cancellation Fee And Early Termination Fee

What is the ADT cancellation fee? If you’re still in your initial contract term when you cancel your ADT security system you’ll be charged 75% of the total remaining monthly service charges. This sum is “payable immediately in full”.

ADT refers to this charge as a “contract termination charge” and goes to some trouble to state that it “is not a penalty”. If ADT cancels your contract during the initial term (because you’ve violated the terms of the contract in some way) then you’ll also be hit with this charge.

### How The ADT Termination Charge Is Calculated

So let’s say you sign up for a 12-month ADT security system contract (like mine) and you want to cancel with 6 months left to run. You’ll be charged 0.75 x 6 = 4.5 months of service charges. In my case, I pay $47.99 a month, so I’d be charged 4.5 x $47.99 = $215.96.

Here is the termination charge you’d pay (based on a $47.99 per month service charge) at various points in a 12-month ADT contract:

| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |

If you paid a security deposit when you first signed up for ADT, you’ll get this money back (less any amounts you owe ADT, including any contract termination charge) within 45 days of your service ending. ADT does not refund any credits of $5 or less that you may have accumulated.

## How To Cancel ADT Without Penalty

Here are some legitimate ways to get out of your ADT contract without paying that nasty 75% termination fee:

* Wait Until You’re Month-To-Month: Once your initial contract term is up, you can cancel at any time without being hit with a contract termination fee.
* Military Relocation: ADT will waive the termination fee for active military personnel who have orders to relocate from the home where the ADT system is installed.
* Object To A Price Increase: ADT is allowed to increase your monthly service charge after the first year of your contract. If you object to this price increase within 30 days of ADT sending you notice, and ADT doesn’t choose to waive the increase, you can terminate your contract 30 days after ADT receives your objection.
* ADT Cancels For Its Own Reasons: If ADT has to cancel your contract for reasons such as its monitoring center being damaged, or if it can no longer maintain the signal connection between your home and its monitoring center, then it will refund any advance payments you made and won’t charge a termination fee.

### Don’t Just Stop Paying

If you stop paying your monthly service charges, ADT will send you a notice saying you have 10 days to “cure” the problem. If you don’t, ADT can end your contract and charge the 75% termination fee. But there’s more. ADT can also demand the entire remaining balance of your contract (not just 75%) all at once, plus any costs it incurs trying to collect the money (such as legal fees), plus interest. Finally, ADT can report you to the credit bureaus. In short, don’t stop paying!

## ADT’s 3 Day Right To Cancel

Within 3 business days of signing your ADT contract, you have the right to cancel it “without any penalty or obligation.” When I signed my contract on January 28, 2026, I had until midnight on January 31, 2026 to cancel. That was a Saturday, which means that Saturdays count as a ‘business day’.

If you live in Alaska you have 5 business days to cancel. New York state residents have 7 business days to cancel a personal emergency response unit and Alabama residents have 30 business days to cancel an alarm or locking system if they are 75 years or older.

To cancel within this window, call 800.327.4348 (a different number to ADT’s main customer service line) or mail (or deliver) the provided Cancellation Form (or any other written notice) to the ADT sales office (printed on the form – in my case this was an office in Aurora, Colorado).

Once ADT receives your cancellation notice, it will refund any payments you made within 10 business days. You have to make your ADT equipment available at your home (in substantially as good condition as when you received it) or if ADT requests, you have to ship the equipment back to ADT at ADT’s expense. If ADT doesn’t pick up the equipment within 20 days of getting your cancellation notice, you can keep it or get rid of it – you have no further obligation to ADT.

## What To Do After You Cancel ADT

The equipment ADT installs in your home is owned by ADT (unless you have a written agreement to the contrary). After your ADT contract ends, ADT can remove, disable or abandon its equipment in your home. It may let you keep the equipment if you make an additional payment (determined by ADT at the time). ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the ADT cancellation number?

To cancel your ADT security system, call 800.238.2727.

### Can you cancel ADT at any time?

Yes, you can cancel your ADT security system at any time but if you’re still in your initial contract term you may have to pay a termination fee.

### Does the ADT contract automatically renew?

Yes, after your initial contract term ends, your ADT contract automatically renews on a month-to-month basis.

### Does ADT have a 3 year contract?

Many ADT contracts are for 36 months.

### Can I cancel ADT if I’m moving?

Yes, you can cancel if you are moving but you may have to pay a termination fee if you are still in your initial contract term.

### Do I have to return the ADT equipment when I cancel?

You don’t own the ADT equipment in your home, ADT does. When you cancel ADT, the company can come and remove the equipment, or it can just ‘abandon’ it. You may be able to buy the equipment from ADT.

### How do I cancel my ADT contract within 3 days of signing?

Within 3 business days of signing your ADT contract you can cancel without penalty. To do this, either call 800.327.4348 or mail or deliver the cancellation form (or any other written notice of cancellation) to the ADT sales office (address printed on the form).
