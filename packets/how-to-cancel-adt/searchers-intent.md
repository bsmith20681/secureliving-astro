# Searcher's Intent: How To Cancel ADT

## Who is searching

Someone who has ADT now and has already decided to leave. They aren't comparing companies yet. They want to know the steps, what it will cost, and how to get through it without being talked out of it.

## Why they're searching

1. **They want the steps.** Most searches are some form of "how to cancel adt" or "cancel adt service." Give the steps first, plainly, before anything else.
2. **They don't want the retention call.** Searches for the cancellation number, canceling online, and canceling "without calling" show they expect ADT to make it hard. Tell them whether they can avoid the call, and if not, what the call is like and what to say.
3. **They're worried about the cost.** Many searches are about the cancellation fee, the early termination fee, canceling "without penalty," and "loopholes." Explain what the fee is, how it's worked out, and the legitimate ways to lower or avoid it.
4. **They don't know where they stand on their contract.** Searches for contract length, the 3 year contract, and automatic renewal show they need to work out whether they're still under contract before they call.
5. **Some just signed and changed their mind.** A few searches ask about the 3 day window to cancel a new contract. Cover it briefly.

## What the article should do

The title should promise more than the bare steps: how to cancel while still under contract, and how to avoid the penalty. Keep "How to Cancel ADT" at the start, then the hook. For example:

- How to Cancel ADT Under Contract Without Paying a Penalty
- How to Cancel ADT Without Penalty, Even Under Contract
- How to Cancel ADT Mid-Contract and Avoid the Termination Fee

The article has to deliver on that promise, so H2s 3 to 5 (contract length, the fee, avoiding the penalty) carry as much weight as the steps.

A reader should finish knowing exactly how to cancel, what it will cost them, and how to keep that cost as low as possible. Answer each worry above in its own section. Use these H2s, in this order:

1. How To Cancel ADT Service, Step By Step (give the ADT cancellation number in the steps, in a normal paragraph, and say what to expect on the call; it doesn't need its own heading)
2. Can You Cancel ADT Online Or Without Calling?
3. How Long Are ADT Contracts?
4. ADT Cancellation Fee And Early Termination Fee
5. How To Cancel ADT Without Penalty
6. ADT's 3 Day Right To Cancel

Stay on canceling. Don't cover what to do with the equipment afterward; a separate article ("using adt equipment after cancellation") does that, so just mention it. Don't recommend a replacement company in detail; end with a short pointer to the ADT alternatives article.
