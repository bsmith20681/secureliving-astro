

# The Ultimate Guide to Using ADT Equipment After Cancellation

The short answer is yes. If you cancel your ADT subscription, you’ll lose access to the ADT app on your phone and the monitoring service, but the alarm, sensors, smoke and CO detectors and Nest cameras will all still work.

This article is for anyone who has just cancelled or is thinking about cancelling their ADT subscription and wants to know what they can still do with their equipment.

## Will My ADT Alarm Still Work If I Cancel?

Yes! If you cancel ADT you can still use your alarm system. You’ll be able to arm and disarm the system and the siren will still sound when the alarm is tripped.

However, you won’t have professional monitoring, so nobody will call the police department when the alarm goes off.

Here’s a quick summary of what still works and what doesn’t:

| Still Works After Cancellation | Stops Working After Cancellation |
| --- | --- |
| Siren still sounds when alarm is tripped | ADT no longer dispatch police |
| Door, window and motion sensors still detect activity | No more push notifications to your phone |
| Smoke and CO detectors still sound off locally | ADT no longer calls the fire dept |
| Can still arm/disarm system with pin code | You lose access to the ADT app on your phone |
| Cameras (if they are Nest cameras) | Can no longer control smart home devices from the app |

### Sensors, Keypad and Siren

All of the sensors in your ADT system (door and window sensors, motion sensors, glass break sensors) will continue to work after you cancel.

You’ll be able to arm and disarm your system the same way as before. You just have to go up to the panel and manually enter your pin code (the same code you used before).

If a sensor is tripped, the siren will still sound. This is still effective as a deterrent because most burglars won’t stick around once they hear the siren.

### Do ADT Smoke and CO Detectors Work Without Service?

Yes, if you cancel ADT your smoke detectors and carbon monoxide detectors will still sound off in your house. However, you don’t get the service where ADT calls the fire department on your behalf.

## Can You Use ADT Cameras Without Service?

Yes, because ADT uses Nest cameras you can still access them through the Google Home app after you cancel.

This is a big plus because companies like SimpliSafe and Vivint use proprietary cameras that stop working when you cancel.

The only exception is if you have an older ADT system that has ADT branded cameras. Those will no longer work after you cancel.

## Does the ADT App Work Without a Subscription?

No. The ADT app (formerly ADT Pulse) does not work without an active subscription.

You won’t be able to remotely arm/disarm your system, receive push notifications when the alarm is tripped, or control your smart home devices from the app.

Smart home devices still work, just not from the app.

## If I Cancel ADT, Do I Keep the Equipment?

According to the ADT contract, all equipment is owned by ADT (unless otherwise agreed to in writing).

But in practice you get to keep the equipment after you cancel.

The only time this doesn’t apply is if you cancel within 3 days of signing up. ADT has a “3 day right to cancel” policy where you can return the equipment and get a full refund.

## Does ADT Remove Equipment After Cancellation?

When you sign up for ADT you agree that they have the right to “remove, disable or abandon” the equipment after you terminate the contract.

In practice ADT just leaves the equipment there.

### How to Return ADT Equipment

This only applies if you are cancelling within the 3 day right to cancel period. The instructions for returning the equipment are:

1. Make the equipment available at your home
2. OR ship the equipment back to ADT (at their expense)

If ADT doesn’t pick up the equipment within 20 days of your “notice of cancellation” then you can keep it. You’ll receive a refund of any payments within 10 business days of ADT receiving your notice of cancellation.

## Can You Convert ADT to Self-Monitoring?

An ADT system without monitoring just means that you can arm and disarm the system at the panel and the siren sounds when the alarm is tripped. That’s it. You don’t get an app or anything.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I remove ADT equipment myself?

Yes. If you want it removed you would need to do it yourself (or hire a handyman). If you have wired sensors I would leave them be (they’re inside the door frames and walls).

### Will ADT buy back equipment?

Yes, at their discretion. You make an additional payment (determined by ADT when you terminate the contract).
