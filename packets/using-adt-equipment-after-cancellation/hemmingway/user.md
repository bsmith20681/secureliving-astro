## Ben's Notes

These are Ben's notes for this article. They are the primary source. Where they disagree with anything below, the notes win.

This article is for someone who has just cancelled or is thinking about cancelling ADT, and they want to know which services will still work and not work with ADT.

They will lose access to the app on their phone and to the monitoring service. This means they can no longer use the app to arm or disarm their system or control their smart home devices from the app. They also lose the monitoring. So if the alarm is tripped then nobody will call the police department for them.

So what still works?

The alarm will still sound. So you can arm and disarm the alarm and it will still sound the alarm if it's tripped. This is good because it can still act as a deterrent if anyone tries to break in. You'll just use the same pin code as before. You'll need to go up to the panel and manually enter the code.

So this means that the sensors also work. Door sensors, motion, and glass break. Smoke and CO detectors also work, but again they don't call out to the fire department. They will still sound off though.

So you can technically still self monitor the alarm. This just means that you can still arm and disarm it all.

The cameras still work since they are Nest cameras and can be accessed through the Google Home app. This is a big plus with ADT since some other companies such as SimpliSafe have proprietary cameras.

You also get to keep the ADT equipment after you cancel, unless you cancel within the 3 day window after having just signed up.

Page 10, "Notice of Cancellation" (the 3 day right to cancel): You may cancel this transaction, without any penalty or obligation, within 3 business days from the date of customer approval. (Alaska: 5 business days.) If you cancel, any payments made by you under the contract will be returned within 10 business days following receipt by the seller of your cancellation notice. If you cancel, you must make available to the seller at your residence, in substantially as good condition as when received, any goods delivered to you under this contract; or you may, if you wish, comply with the instructions of the seller regarding the return shipment of the goods at the seller's expense and risk. If you do make the goods available to the seller and the seller does not pick them up within 20 days of the date of your notice of cancellation, you may retain or dispose of the goods without any further obligation. If you fail to make the goods available to the seller, or if you agree to return the goods to the seller and fail to do so, then you remain liable for performance of all obligations under the contract. To cancel this transaction, call 800.327.4348, or mail or deliver the cancellation notice or any other written notice to the ADT sales office printed on the form.

Other contract facts: the initial term is 12 months if you paid for the equipment upfront (Ben's case) or 36 months if you financed it. After the initial term it renews month to month in 30 day terms. Ben pays $47.99 a month on a 12 month agreement.

## Excerpts From Ben's Earlier Articles

From "How to Cancel ADT": "The contract states that after your contract ends, ADT can remove, disable or even 'abandon' its equipment in your home. More often than not, you keep the equipment after cancelling. You get to keep the cameras too. This means you can arm and disarm the system like normal and the siren will still sound if the alarm is tripped. However, nobody from the monitoring station will contact you or your local police department. If someone breaks in while your ADT alarm is armed, the siren will still go off. And honestly, most burglars aren't going to stick around when they hear that. The siren alone is a solid deterrent."

What still works without service (same article): the siren still sounds when the alarm is triggered; door, window, and motion sensors still detect activity; smoke detectors and CO detectors still sound locally; you can arm and disarm the system with the pin code; smart home devices still function, just not from the app.

What you lose: no access to the ADT app on your phone; ADT won't dispatch police, fire, or medical services; no remote arm/disarm from your phone.

Same article on the app: "The ADT app does not work without an active subscription. This is one of the most common misconceptions I see. People assume that because the physical equipment still functions, the app should too. But ADT requires a paid monitoring plan to access the app. Without a subscription, you can't remotely arm or disarm the system, receive push notifications, or control smart home devices."

Same article on cameras: "If the cameras are from Nest, then you can use those cameras without ADT service. You'll need to use the Google Home app to connect the cameras and you can watch them from there. If there are older ADT branded cameras then unfortunately those will not work on their own and you will need to activate the service in order to use them."

Same article on removal: "ADT doesn't come repossess the sensors, panel, or wiring when someone cancels. It's all still there." And: "ADT generally does not come out to remove old equipment after cancellation. The equipment stays in the home. If you want it removed, you would need to do it yourself or hire a handyman. Wired sensors are usually best left in place since they are inside the door frames and walls." Also: "The equipment doesn't have an expiration date or anything like that. I've seen homes with ADT systems that are 10+ years old and the sensors still work fine. The batteries in the sensors will need replacing eventually, but the equipment itself holds up."

Same article on other companies: "Most other companies can use existing sensors and smart home equipment, but they cannot use the panel or cameras. Other larger companies such as Vivint can typically use ADT equipment, but if you purchase a DIY system such as SimpliSafe or Cove then neither of those systems will be able to use any of the ADT equipment."

From Ben's ADT review: ADT now uses Google Nest cameras across the lineup. Ben's setup has a Nest Doorbell and a Nest outdoor camera. Even with service you watch the Nest feeds through the Google Home app, not the ADT app, and you can't watch them on the Command panel. For a while ADT used its own proprietary cameras; the camera icon on the panel only works with those older cameras. Ben's setup: touchscreen Command panel, Nest doorbell, Nest outdoor camera, five door sensors, two glass break sensors, two motion sensors. ## Company Data

### ADT
- Professional install, contract required: 12 month minimum if you buy the equipment upfront, up to 36 months if you finance it. If you finance, you keep the service as long as there's a balance on the equipment.
- Monthly: $42.99 for monitoring and the app, $48.99 with cameras.
- Equipment from $369.
- Panel: touchscreen Command Panel. Sensors: door/window, motion, glass break. Keyfob, siren.
- Cameras: Nest doorbell, indoor, outdoor. Not proprietary to the service: Nest cameras keep working through the Google Home app if you cancel.
- Smart home: Z-Wave, door locks, thermostats, garage doors, lights. Nest compatible.
- Pros listed on the site: top of the line equipment, largest and longest running security company, cameras still work if you cancel service, excellent mobile app, touchscreen keypad.
- Cons: higher monthly cost, minimum 12 month agreement, equipment can be expensive upfront.

### SimpliSafe
- DIY, month to month, no contract.
- Cameras are proprietary and stop working if you cancel.

### Vivint
- Professional install. Month to month if you pay for the equipment upfront; if you finance (42 or 60 months at 0% APR) you keep the service as long as there's a balance.
- Monitoring from $29.99 a month. Equipment from $599.
- Cameras are proprietary and stop working if you cancel.
- Ben's top ADT alternative.
