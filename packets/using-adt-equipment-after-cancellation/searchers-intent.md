# Searcher's Intent: Using ADT Equipment After Cancellation

This article is for someone he either has just cancelled or is thinking about cancelling ADT and they want to know which services will still work and not work with ADT. 

They will lose access to the app on their phone and to monitoring service. This means they can no longer use the app to arm or disarm their system or control their smart home devices from the app. They also lose on the monitoring. So if the alarm is tripped then nobody will call the police department for them. 

So what still works?

The alarm will still sound. So you can arm and disarm the alarm and it will still sound the alarm if it's tripped. This is good because it can still act a deterrent if anyone tries to break in. You'll just use the same pin code as before. You'll need to go up to it and manually enter the code. 

So this means that the sensors also work. Door sensors motion and glass break. Smoke and CO detectors also work, but again they don't call out to the fire department. It will still sound off though. 

So you can technically still self monitor the alarm. This just means that you can still arm and disarm it all. 

The cameras still work since they are nest cameras and can be access through the Google home app. This is a big plus with ADT since some other companies such as SimpliSafe have properitery cameras. 

You also get to keep the ADT equipment after you cancel unless you cancel within the 3 day window after having just signed up.
