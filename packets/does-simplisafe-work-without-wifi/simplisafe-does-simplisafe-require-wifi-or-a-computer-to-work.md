# Does SimpliSafe® require Wi-Fi or a Computer to Work?

Although you do not need a computer or Wi-Fi for SimpliSafe® to work for your basic home security needs, **access to Wi-Fi and a Smartphone and/or computer are strongly recommended** to best take advantage of all of your system’s security features.

## Why Wi-Fi?

A Wi-Fi connection ensures that you and your home are always protected and up to date. Connecting your system to Wi-Fi provides a secondary connection source, in addition to the cellular connection that works when subscribed to a professional monitoring plan. Both connection methods ensure your system can connect to our Monitoring Center in the event of an alarm. Wi-Fi also allows your system to retrieve and download all new firmware updates to keep your system and its devices working properly and at their best.

Additionally, there are many key features of a SimpliSafe® system that you will not have access to without a Wi-Fi connection. **Wi-Fi allows you to:**

-   Use SimpliSafe® cameras
    
    -   Install and connect the cameras to your system
        
    -   Stream live video from our cameras
        
    -   View and download recordings\*
        
    -   Enable Visual Verification\*
        
-   Arm your system with Google Assistant or Alexa\*
    
-   Retrieve an download necessary firmware updates for your system and devices
    

_​_[**_\*Available on select monitoring plans_** (opens in new tab)](https://support.simplisafe.com/page/monitoring-plans)

## What if I can’t access Wi-Fi?

Without Wi-Fi, you can still change your system and sensors’ basic settings **via the Keypad** as well as take advantage of our Professional Monitoring service’s basic features. And if you are [**subscribed to a monitoring plan that includes built-in network backup** (opens in new tab)](https://support.simplisafe.com/articles/alarm-events-monitoring/what-are-the-service-plan-options/6344794a013ba90af0bce6a4), your Base Station will still communicate with our monitoring center using its built-in network backup connection.
