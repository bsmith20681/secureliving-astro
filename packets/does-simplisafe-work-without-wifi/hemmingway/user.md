# Does SimpliSafe Work Without WiFi? What Still Works (2026)

## The short answer

**Yes, SimpliSafe works without WiFi - but only if you have a professional monitoring plan.** Every SimpliSafe monitoring plan includes a built-in **cellular connection** in the Base Station, which takes over when WiFi is down or was never set up. Your sensors keep talking to the Base Station over their own radio signal (not WiFi), the siren still sounds, and the monitoring center still gets alarm signals and can dispatch police.

The catch: **without WiFi you lose cameras, most app control, and firmware updates** - and if you have _no monitoring plan at all_, a WiFi-less SimpliSafe becomes little more than a local siren. Here's the full breakdown.

## How SimpliSafe connects (why WiFi isn't required)

![Two devices side by side on a console table showing a primary WiFi router and a separate cellular backup base station](https://compareyoursecurity.com/assets/wifi-vs-cellular-setup.jpg)

Two separate connections: your WiFi router (primary) and the Base Station's own cellular backup.

A SimpliSafe system has three separate connections, and mixing them up is what confuses most buyers:

-   **Sensor radio:** door/window sensors, motion sensors and keypads talk to the Base Station over their own proprietary radio frequency - _never_ WiFi. This works with no internet at all.
-   **WiFi:** the Base Station's primary link to the outside world - app control, camera video, firmware updates.
-   **Cellular backup:** included with every professional monitoring plan. When WiFi drops, the Base Station automatically switches to the cellular network to reach the monitoring center. If you never had WiFi, it simply uses cellular full-time.

The Base Station also has a **rechargeable backup battery (up to ~24 hours)**, so a power cut doesn't kill the system either.

## What still works without WiFi

![Base Station glowing green on a table near a window at dusk with a cellular tower visible in the distance outside](https://compareyoursecurity.com/assets/cellular-tower-backup.jpg)

When WiFi drops, the Base Station switches to cellular automatically - no action needed.

With a monitoring plan (cellular active), no WiFi means you keep:

-   **24/7 professional monitoring** - alarm signals reach the monitoring center over cellular, and they can dispatch emergency services.
-   **All sensors and the siren** - entry sensors, motion sensors, glass-break, smoke/CO alerts all trigger normally.
-   **Arming/disarming at the keypad** - plus basic settings changes directly on the keypad.
-   **Power-outage protection** - the Base Station battery keeps it alive up to ~24 hours.

In other words: the _security core_ of the system survives without internet. That's the whole point of cellular backup - burglars can't defeat the system by cutting your internet.

![Living room at night with lamps glowing and a keypad lit up on the wall, illustrating power-outage battery backup](https://compareyoursecurity.com/assets/battery-backup-night.jpg)

Power out, WiFi down - the Base Station's own battery and cellular link keep monitoring active.

## What stops working without WiFi

![Indoor security camera on a bookshelf with a WiFi router visible in the background of the living room](https://compareyoursecurity.com/assets/camera-needs-wifi.jpg)

Cameras are the one piece that always needs WiFi - cellular backup doesn't cover video.

Be realistic about what you give up:

-   **Cameras and video doorbells** - all SimpliSafe cameras require WiFi. No WiFi = no live view, no recordings, no video verification.
-   **Most app features** - remote arm/disarm, notifications and settings from your phone depend on the Base Station having internet. Over cellular-only, app functionality is limited to basics at best.
-   **Firmware updates** - the system can't download updates, so you miss fixes and improvements over time.
-   **Self-monitoring alerts** - if you skip the monitoring plan and rely on free app alerts, those alerts need WiFi. No plan + no WiFi = sensors still trigger the siren, but _nobody gets notified_.

## Real-world scenarios

![Woman arming her security system at the keypad by the front door](https://compareyoursecurity.com/assets/keypad-arming-door.jpg)

Arming and disarming at the keypad works the same whether WiFi is up or down.

**Rural home / no internet service:** workable. Buy the system, take a monitoring plan, and it runs on cellular full-time - just skip the cameras. Check that your address has decent cellular coverage first.

**Internet outage at a normal home:** nothing to do - cellular backup kicks in automatically, monitoring continues, and WiFi features return when your internet does.

**Renter without a router:** the alarm side works fine on a monitoring plan. If you want cameras, you'd need at least a phone hotspot - or consider a camera that records locally instead (see our [wired vs wireless camera guide](https://compareyoursecurity.com/blog/wired-vs-wireless-security-cameras.html)).

**No monitoring plan, no WiFi:** honestly not recommended. You get a loud local siren and nothing else - no dispatch, no alerts, no record of what happened.

## SimpliSafe WiFi FAQs

### Does SimpliSafe need WiFi to be installed?

Initial setup is much easier with WiFi (app-guided), but a monitoring plan's cellular connection can carry the system day to day. If you have zero internet, plan for setup at a location with WiFi or ask SimpliSafe support for cellular-only activation guidance.

### Do SimpliSafe sensors use WiFi?

No. Sensors communicate with the Base Station over their own radio frequency, which works with no internet at all.

### Does the alarm still call police without WiFi?

With a professional monitoring plan, yes - the Base Station reaches the monitoring center over cellular, and they dispatch. Without a plan, nobody is notified.

### Do SimpliSafe cameras work without WiFi?

No. All SimpliSafe cameras and doorbells require a WiFi connection for video.

### What happens during a power outage?

The Base Station's rechargeable battery keeps the system running up to about 24 hours, and cellular keeps monitoring connected if your router is down.
