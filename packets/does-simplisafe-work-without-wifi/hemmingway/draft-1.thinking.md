# Does SimpliSafe Work Without WiFi? Here’s What Does

Yes, SimpliSafe’s alarm and monitoring works without WiFi thanks to its built-in cellular backup. However, SimpliSafe’s cameras require WiFi in order to stream video to your phone and record footage.

Even if you never hook up your WiFi, the SimpliSafe system will work. It’ll just operate over its cellular connection the entire time.

## How SimpliSafe Stays Connected Without WiFi

The SimpliSafe system operates primarily over WiFi. But it also has a built-in cellular backup (assuming you have a SimpliSafe monitoring plan that includes cellular backup).

Any time your WiFi connection drops, the SimpliSafe system switches to the cellular backup to maintain its connection to SimpliSafe’s monitoring center.

### The Base Station Is Where The Magic Happens

The base station is the central hub that connects all your sensors, cameras, and other devices. It’s also the piece of equipment that communicates with SimpliSafe’s monitoring center.

So even though your sensors aren’t connected to WiFi, they communicate with the base station (via radio frequency). And the base station, if it can’t use your WiFi, will use its cellular backup connection.

Keep in mind that the SimpliSafe base station doesn’t include cellular backup if you’re on the free monitoring plan.

## What Works And What Stops Working Without WiFi

Here’s a summary of what works without WiFi and what doesn’t:

So if you have a monitored SimpliSafe system, you don’t need to worry about what happens if your WiFi goes down. You’ll still get professional monitoring.

## Do SimpliSafe Cameras Work Without WiFi?

No, SimpliSafe cameras do not work without WiFi.

In fact, the cameras are heavily reliant on WiFi. Without WiFi you won’t be able to stream video to your phone, and they won’t record any footage.

The cameras do have a backup battery, so they’ll keep working during a power outage. But that’s not going to help you if you don’t have WiFi.

## Can You Set Up SimpliSafe Without WiFi?

Unfortunately, the answer is no. You need to connect the base station to your WiFi network during the set-up process.

And after that, you’re going to use the SimpliSafe app to connect all the sensors and cameras.

## What Happens When You Have A Power Outage?

The SimpliSafe base station has a 24-hour battery backup. So in the event of a power outage, your system will remain active and be able to communicate with the monitoring center via its cellular connection.

## What SimpliSafe Monitoring Plan Do You Need For Cellular Backup?

There are a lot of SimpliSafe monitoring plans to choose from. You can even use the system without a monthly monitoring plan.

But if you want professional monitoring and cellular backup you need to subscribe to the Standard Monitoring or higher tier plan.

Personally, I think the sweet spot is the Core plan. It gives you all the benefits of the Standard plan and tacks on a few extras like camera cloud storage, Intruder Intervention, video verification, and a lifetime warranty on all SimpliSafe equipment.

## Is SimpliSafe A Good Security System If You Have Unreliable Internet?

If you want a professionally monitored security system, and you don’t have reliable internet service (or any at all), SimpliSafe is a great option.

But if you’re hoping to use security cameras, you’re going to need a reliable WiFi connection.

The alarm and monitoring side of SimpliSafe works without Wi-Fi. That’s because it has a cellular backup connection that takes over when your internet goes out. But the cameras do not work without Wi-Fi.

Yes. As long as you have a monitoring plan that includes a cellular connection (Standard monitoring and higher).

### Does SimpliSafe Work Without Internet?

Yes, the security system works without an internet connection. You can arm and disarm from the keypad, the siren sounds, and the monitoring center is alerted when the alarm triggers. The only things that stop working are the cameras and the mobile app.

### Do SimpliSafe Cameras Work Without Wi-Fi?

No. All SimpliSafe cameras require a Wi-Fi connection in order to stream or record video.

### Can You Set Up SimpliSafe Without Wi-Fi?

No. You need to have Wi-Fi in order to set up the system.

### How Reliable Is SimpliSafe During Power Outages?
