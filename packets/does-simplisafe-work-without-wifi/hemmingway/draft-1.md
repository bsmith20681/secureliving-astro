

When you don’t have WiFi (or ever did), does the SimpliSafe alarm system still protect your house?

Yes, it does. Even if your WiFi is down, the alarm and professional monitoring will continue to work. That’s because the system has a built-in cellular backup that kicks in when it can’t connect to the internet.

Unfortunately, the SimpliSafe cameras won’t work without WiFi.

## How SimpliSafe Works Without WiFi

The SimpliSafe system uses your WiFi connection to communicate with SimpliSafe’s monitoring center. But it also has a cellular backup (on all plans except the free plan).

If your internet service goes down, the base station will switch to the cellular backup. This allows the system to continue to function and communicate with the monitoring center.

If you don’t have an internet service, the system will just operate over the cellular network all the time.

## What Still Works When You Don’t Have WiFi

When your WiFi is down, you’ll lose some functionality. But the core parts of the system (sensors, base station, siren) will keep working.

Here’s a list of what still works and what doesn’t:

* Arm and disarm from the keypad
* Sensors (door/window, motion, etc)
* Siren sounds when alarm is triggered
* Professional monitoring (monitoring plans with cellular backup)
* System does NOT arm or disarm from app
* You won’t receive text or app alerts
* Cameras won’t record or stream video

## Do SimpliSafe Cameras Work Without WiFi?

No, the SimpliSafe cameras require a WiFi connection. Without WiFi you won’t be able to view live video from your phone or record any footage.

SimpliSafe only offers cloud storage for video footage. So the cameras have to upload the footage to the cloud when it’s recorded.

## Can You Set Up SimpliSafe Without WiFi?

No. You need to use the app to set up the system. And you have to connect the base station to your WiFi network before you can start adding sensors and cameras.

## What Happens During A Power Outage?

SimpliSafe’s base station comes with a 24-hour battery backup. So if you have a power outage and your WiFi router shuts down, the system will continue to operate.

It’ll use the backup battery to stay powered and the cellular connection to communicate with the monitoring center.

## What Monitoring Plan Do You Need For Cellular Backup?

If you want cellular backup, you’re going to need a professional monitoring plan. You get that with the Standard Monitoring plan and higher.

SimpliSafe does have a free monitoring plan. But with this plan you won’t have access to professional monitoring or cellular backup.

## Is SimpliSafe A Good System If Your Internet Is Unreliable?

If you want a professionally monitored alarm system and you don’t have reliable internet service, I definitely think SimpliSafe is worth considering.

The only drawback is that you won’t be able to use the security cameras.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does SimpliSafe work without Wi-Fi?

The alarm and monitoring side of SimpliSafe works without Wi-Fi. It has a cellular backup connection that automatically takes over when your internet service goes out. But the SimpliSafe cameras do not work without Wi-Fi.

### Does SimpliSafe have cellular backup?

Yes, as long as you have a monitoring plan that includes a cellular connection (Standard Monitoring and above).

### Does SimpliSafe work without internet?

Yes. You can arm and disarm the system from the keypad, the siren sounds, and SimpliSafe’s monitoring center is alerted when the alarm triggers. You just won’t be able to use the app or the cameras.

### Do SimpliSafe cameras work without Wi-Fi?

No. All SimpliSafe cameras require a Wi-Fi connection to stream or record video.

### Can you set up SimpliSafe without Wi-Fi?

No, you have to have Wi-Fi to set up the system.

### How reliable is SimpliSafe during power outages?

The base station has a 24-hour battery backup and cellular connection. So even if the power and the internet go out at the same time, your system will stay active.

### Does SimpliSafe work without power?

Yes (as long as you have a monitoring plan with a cellular connection). The system has a 24-hour battery backup.
