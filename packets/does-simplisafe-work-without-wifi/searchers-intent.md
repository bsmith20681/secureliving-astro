SimpliSafe does work without Wifi. It has a cellular backup so even if your wifi or power goes out you'll still have monitoring. 

You need need simplisafe to set it up though. Since you'll need it to connect the base station to your wifi and then use the app to connect the sensors and cameras. 

The cameras do not work without wifi, but can work without power since they have a backup battery in them. 

They cameras will not be able to stream to your phone or record unless they have wifi.
