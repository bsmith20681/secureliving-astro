# Searcher's Intent: How To Cancel Vivint

## Who is searching

Someone who has Vivint now and has already decided to leave. Most are still paying off financed equipment, so they're under a contract that can run up to five years. They want the steps, what it will cost, and whether there's any way to get out without paying off the whole balance.

## Why they're searching

1. **They want the steps.** The biggest searches are "cancel vivint," "how to cancel vivint" and "vivint cancellation." Give the steps first, plainly: who to contact, the cancellation number, the documents email, and what goes in the Notice of Cancellation. The two competitors disagree here (one says email a written notice 30 days ahead, the other says call ext. 5020), so the article has to say clearly which one works and verify the numbers before publishing.
2. **They're worried about the cost.** Many searches ask about the cancellation fee, the early termination fee, canceling "without penalty," and canceling "before paying off equipment." Explain that the cost is mostly the remaining equipment balance, when monitoring fees for the rest of the term can also apply, and that it depends on the contract they signed.
3. **They want a way out without the penalty.** "How to get out of vivint contract," "without penalty" and the military searches show people looking for exceptions. Cover the ones Vivint reviews case by case: death, bankruptcy, moving to assisted living, military deployment, PCS orders, medical discharge or retirement, and a contract takeover by someone else.
4. **They don't know where they stand on their contract.** Searches for contract length, "does vivint have a contract," and auto renewal show they need to check whether they're still under contract, and how long is left, before they cancel.
5. **Some just signed and changed their mind.** "Cancel vivint within 3 days," "before installation" and the notice of cancellation form searches are about the 3 day right to cancel. Cover it briefly.
6. **They expect it to be hard.** "Vivint won't let me cancel," "why is it so hard to cancel vivint" and the complaints searches show they expect a retention push and surprise bills afterward. Say what to expect and how to get the cancellation confirmed in writing.
7. **They wonder what happens to the equipment.** A few searches ask about returning equipment and whether the cameras still work after canceling. Answer it in a short section; there's no separate article for it in this cluster.

## What the article should do

The title should promise more than the bare steps: how to cancel while still under contract, and how to avoid or lower the penalty. Keep "How to Cancel Vivint" at the start, then the hook. For example:

- How to Cancel Vivint Under Contract Without Paying a Penalty
- How to Cancel Vivint and Get Out of Your Contract Early

A reader should finish knowing exactly how to cancel, what it will cost them, and whether they qualify for a way out. Answer each worry above in its own section. Suggested H2s, in this order:

1. How To Cancel Vivint, Step By Step (cancellation number, documents email and what to put in the notice, in normal paragraphs)
2. How Long Are Vivint Contracts?
3. Vivint Cancellation Fee And Early Termination Fee
4. How To Cancel Vivint Without Penalty
5. Vivint's 3 Day Right To Cancel
6. What Happens To Your Vivint Equipment After You Cancel

Moving is a reason people cancel, so mention briefly that Vivint can move the system and that a contract can be transferred. Don't make it a section.

Stay on canceling. Don't recommend a replacement company in detail; end with a short pointer to the Vivint alternatives pillar. Removing the doorbell has its own article ("how to remove vivint doorbell"), so only link to it.
