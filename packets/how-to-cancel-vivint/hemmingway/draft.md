

# How To Cancel Vivint (And Get Out of a Contract)

You’ve made up your mind that you no longer want Vivint. Now it’s time to start the process of cancelling.

## How To Cancel Vivint Service

Here are the steps to follow:

1. Review your Vivint agreement. Find out when your “initial term” ends and how much you still owe on your equipment.
2. Call Vivint Customer Support (1-800-678-2635) to begin the cancellation process.
3. Ask Vivint for your “payoff amount”. This is the amount you will have to pay to cancel your contract.
4. Put your cancellation request in writing and send it to Vivint at least 30 days before you want your service to be terminated.
5. Get written confirmation from Vivint that they’ve received your cancellation request.
6. After you cancel, monitor your account to make sure you’re not being charged for service you don’t have.

Your written notice should include:

* Date of letter
* Your Vivint service number
* A clear statement that you want to cancel your contract
* A brief explanation of why you are canceling
* Your signature

### How To Cancel Vivint Online

You can’t cancel Vivint from their mobile app or from your online account. The Vivint agreement requires you to submit “Written Notice” if you want to cancel. Print out a copy of the cancellation form and send it by mail (or deliver it in person).

## Does Vivint Have A Contract?

Only if you choose to finance your equipment. If you pay up front for your equipment your service is month to month and you can cancel at any time without penalty. (That’s what I did when I bought my system.)

### How Long Is A Vivint Contract?

If you choose to finance your equipment with Vivint you can get 0% APR for 42 or 60 months. You have to continue to have service from Vivint for as long as you have a balance on your equipment. You can’t just cancel your monitoring service and continue to make payments on your equipment.

The Vivint agreement says “The initial term starts on the day this agreement is signed.” After the initial term ends your service is month to month and “either party may terminate this agreement at any time after the initial term upon at least thirty (30) days’ prior written notice to the other party.”

Also be aware that after your initial term ends, Vivint can increase your monthly professional monitoring fee. (You have the right to cancel your service with 30 days notice if they do this.)

## Does Vivint Have A Cancellation Fee?

There is no set cancellation fee with Vivint. If you financed your equipment you will owe whatever remaining balance is left. The exact amount you owe when you cancel will depend on the terms of the contract you signed. Some Vivint customers have said that they were required to pay Vivint the monthly monitoring fees for the remaining length of their contract when they cancelled. Ask Vivint for your payoff amount in writing.

### Can You Cancel Vivint Before Paying Off Equipment?

No. If you want to cancel your Vivint service before you pay off your equipment you have to pay the remaining balance. From the Vivint agreement:

> “If, at any point during the initial term of this Contract, you fulfill your obligations to pay all amounts owing for the purchase of any equipment pursuant to the SES, then you may terminate this Agreement without penalty or early termination fee with thirty (30) days prior written notice.”

## How To Get Out Of A Vivint Contract Without Penalty

There are a few ways to get out of a Vivint contract without having to pay off your equipment:

* Pay off your equipment early
* Wait until your “initial term” is over
* If you are in one of the following situations call Vivint and ask them to review your contract:  
   - Death of the account holder  
   - Bankruptcy  
   - Moving to an assisted living facility  
   - You are in the military

Vivint’s Exceptions Review Team will review your request case by case. They will ask you for documentation. Calling Vivint to request an exception does not guarantee that they will release you from your contract.

Vivint will waive any early termination fee or cancellation fee for active U.S. military personnel (or anyone who has been discharged in the last 12 months) who are:

* Being deployed for 90 days or longer
* Required to move locations (even if it is the same base)
* Retiring after 20 or more years of service
* Being medically discharged and are moving residences

Other veterans and their dependents are not eligible. You will need to provide current orders or other official military documents.

There is also a deferral program for military personnel who are on temporary assignment away from their permanent residence for less than 12 months. If you qualify for this program Vivint will suspend your agreement and the term of your contract will not run while it is suspended.

A regular (non-medical) military discharge is not grounds to cancel your contract. But if you want to move your system or transfer your agreement to a friend or family member Vivint will waive the fee.

You can also transfer your Vivint contract to another person. You’ll need to get approval from Vivint first.

## How To Cancel Vivint Within 3 Days

Vivint has what is called a “buyer’s right to cancel” clause. This allows you to cancel within three days under the following conditions:

> “THIS IS A HOME SOLICITATION SALE, AND IF YOU DO NOT WANT THE GOODS OR SERVICES, YOU MAY CANCEL THIS AGREEMENT BY PROVIDING WRITTEN NOTICE TO THE SELLER IN PERSON, BY TELEGRAM, OR BY MAIL. THIS NOTICE MUST INDICATE THAT YOU DO NOT WANT THE GOODS OR SERVICES AND MUST BE DELIVERED OR POST MARKED BEFORE MIDNIGHT ON THE THIRD BUSINESS DAY AFTER YOU SIGN THIS AGREEMENT.”

Keep in mind that the three day count begins when you sign the contract (not when your system is installed).

## Why Is It So Hard To Cancel Vivint?

The main reason it is difficult to cancel Vivint is because most customers finance their equipment. As long as you have a balance on your equipment you have to have service from Vivint. They also require 30 days written notice before your service is disconnected. When you call to cancel expect the person you speak with to offer you something to stay (a discount, free equipment, etc.). If you agree to any offer get it in writing. A common complaint about cancelling Vivint is that customers continue to get bills after they cancel.

## If I Cancel Vivint Will My Equipment Still Work?

If you cancel your service with Vivint here is what will happen:

You lose access to the Vivint app. You can no longer watch your cameras or download video clips from your phone. (Your cameras will continue to record locally to the Vivint panel.) Your alarm system will still arm and disarm and you will get a siren if there is an alarm. But the system is no longer being monitored (no one will call the police, fire department or ambulance). The keypad on your door lock and thermostat will work (as will the controls on the Vivint panel) but you can no longer control them from the app and you lose any smart home “automations” that you had set up.

Vivint cameras are proprietary – you can’t take them and hook them up to another alarm company. If you are moving houses Vivint has a program where they will move your system.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I Cancel Vivint Anytime?

If you paid up front for your equipment yes, you can cancel anytime. If you are financing your equipment you can cancel at any time but you will have to pay the remaining balance on your equipment.

### Can I Cancel Vivint If I’m Still Under Contract?

Yes, but you will have to pay the remaining balance on your equipment.

### What Happens If I Cancel Vivint?

When you sign the Vivint agreement you authorize them to “remotely disconnect your communicator from the monitoring center and remotely disconnect the communications/system software.”

### If I Cancel Vivint Will I Lose Everything?

It depends on what you are trying to do. The Vivint agreement says “the system may not work with equipment used by other alarm companies or monitoring centers.”

### Can You Cancel Vivint After Paying Off Equipment?

Yes. The Vivint agreement says “you may terminate this Agreement without penalty or early termination fee with thirty (30) days prior written notice” after you pay off your equipment.

### Does Vivint Require A Contract?

If you choose to finance your equipment with Vivint then yes. But you can also pay up front for your equipment in which case you are month to month and can cancel at any time.
