# How to Cancel Vivint (And Get Out of a Contract)

If you want to cancel your Vivint security service, here’s what you need to do.

## How To Cancel Vivint Service

1. Check your contract to determine the length of your “initial term” and how much you still owe on your equipment.
2. Call Vivint customer support at 1-800-678-2635 to initiate the cancellation process.
3. Determine your payoff amount. This will normally be the balance you still owe on your equipment.
4. Send a written notification of your intent to cancel your Vivint service at least 30 days in advance of your desired cancellation date. Ask your Vivint rep where to send this notice.
5. Get confirmation in writing that Vivint has accepted your cancellation request and the date that your service will be terminated.
6. Check your statement after you cancel to make sure you’re not being charged for service you no longer have.

Here’s what you should include in your written notice:

* Date of the letter
* Your Vivint service number
* A clear indication that you want to cancel your contract
* A short explanation of why you are cancelling (optional)
* Your signature

### How To Cancel Vivint Online

You cannot cancel Vivint in their mobile app or online account. The Vivint agreement says “Written Notice” is required to cancel. The easiest way to do this is to print out the cancellation form and send it by mail, although you can also deliver it in person or by telegram.

## Does Vivint Have A Contract?

Only if you finance your equipment. If you pay up front for your equipment you’re month to month and can cancel at any time without penalty. That’s what I did when I got Vivint in my house.

### How Long Is A Vivint Contract?

Vivint will finance your equipment at 0% APR for 42 or 60 months. You’re required to have Vivint service as long as there is a balance on your equipment. The Vivint agreement says “The initial term starts on the day this agreement is signed”. After that you are month to month and can terminate at any time with 30 days written notice.

Note that after the initial term Vivint has the right to increase your monthly services fee, “subject to your right to cancel on 30 days notice.”

## Does Vivint Have A Cancellation Fee?

There is no flat cancellation fee. If you financed your equipment you will have to pay whatever you still owe. Depending on the contract you signed some Vivint customers have also been required to pay the monthly monitoring fees for the rest of the contract term when they cancel. I recommend asking for the payoff figure in writing.

### Can You Cancel Vivint Before Paying Off Equipment?

Unfortunately the answer is no. You have to pay the remaining balance on your equipment. Here’s what the Vivint agreement says:

“If at any point during the initial term of this contract you fulfill your obligations to pay all amounts owing for the purchase of any equipment pursuant to the schedule of equipment and services (SES) then you may terminate this agreement without penalty or early termination fee with 30 days prior written notice.”

## How To Get Out Of A Vivint Contract Without Penalty

There are a few ways to get out of a Vivint contract without paying off your equipment:

* Pay off your equipment early
* Wait until the “initial term” is over
* If any of the following apply to you, call Vivint and ask them to review your contract:
  * Death of the account holder
  * Bankruptcy
  * Moving to an assisted living facility
  * You are in the military (more on this below)

Vivint has a special program for active US military personnel (or those who have been discharged in the last 12 months) who are:

* Being deployed for 90 days or more
* Required to move locations (even if it’s the same base)
* Retiring after serving 20+ years
* Being medically discharged and moving residences

Other veterans and military dependents are not eligible for this program. You’ll need to provide current orders or other military documents in order to qualify. There is also a deferral program for military personnel who are on temporary assignment away from their permanent residence for less than 12 months. A regular military discharge is not grounds to cancel your contract, but Vivint will waive the fee to move your system or transfer the agreement to a family member or friend.

You can also transfer your Vivint agreement to someone else. This is a good option if you are selling your house and don’t want to take your system with you. Vivint’s approval is required.

## How To Cancel Vivint Within 3 Days

Vivint has a “Buyer’s Right to Cancel” clause that lets you cancel within three days. Here is the exact language from the Vivint agreement:

“This is a home solicitation sale and if you do not want the goods or services you may cancel this agreement by providing written notice to the seller in person, by telegram or by mail. This notice must indicate that you do not want the goods or services and must be delivered or postmarked before midnight on the third business day after you sign this agreement.”

Note that the 3 day clock starts when you sign the contract and not when your system is installed.

## Why Is It So Hard To Cancel Vivint?

Most people who have Vivint are financing their equipment and their service is tied to the remaining balance. Vivint also requires a written notification 30 days in advance of your cancellation. When you call to cancel they are going to try to talk you out of it. They may offer you a discount or some new piece of equipment. Get anything they agree to in writing. A lot of people complain that they continue to get bills from Vivint after they cancel.

## If I Cancel Vivint Will My Equipment Still Work?

If you cancel your service with Vivint you will lose access to the Vivint app which means you cannot watch your cameras or download video clips from your phone. Your cameras will continue to record locally to the Vivint panel but you can only view them at the panel. Your alarm system will still arm and disarm and sound a siren but it won’t be monitored (no one will call the police, fire department or an ambulance). The keypad for your door lock and thermostat will still work as will the controls on the Vivint panel, but you won’t be able to control them from the app and you lose any “automations” you have set up.

Vivint cameras are proprietary so you can’t take them and use them with another alarm company. If you are moving house Vivint will move your system for you. Check out my article on Vivint alternatives if you’re in the market for a new system. I recommend ADT if you want the same features as Vivint and Cove if you want to pay less.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I Cancel Vivint Anytime?

Yes, as long as you paid up front for your equipment. If you are financing your equipment you’ll have to pay the remaining balance to cancel.

### Can I Cancel Vivint If I’m Still Under Contract?

Yes, but you’ll have to pay the remaining balance on your equipment.

### What Happens If I Cancel Vivint?

When you sign the Vivint agreement you authorize them to “remotely disconnect the communicator from the monitoring center and remotely disconnect the communications/system software.”

### If I Cancel Vivint Will I Lose Everything?

According to the Vivint agreement “the system may not work with equipment used by other alarm companies or monitoring centers.”

### Can You Cancel Vivint After Paying Off Equipment?

Yes, you can terminate your agreement “without penalty or early termination fee” with 30 days written notice.

### Does Vivint Require A Contract?

Only if you choose to finance your equipment. If you pay up front for your equipment you can cancel at any time.
