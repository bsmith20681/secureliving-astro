SimpliSafe offers a flexible alternative to traditional security contracts. The system can be installed without professional help and includes optional professional monitoring.

### **4\. Ring**, best for camera-focused smart home security

Owned by Amazon, Ring is widely known for its video doorbells and camera ecosystem. The platform also offers a DIY home alarm system and optional professional monitoring.

Many homeowners begin with Ring doorbells or outdoor cameras and later expand into the Ring Alarm system with sensors and monitoring. The system can be managed through the Ring mobile app and integrates tightly with Amazon Alexa devices.

**Best for:** Homeowners who want a camera-first DIY security system with smart-home integrations.

**Reputation:** Ring ranked second in professionally monitored systems and first in self-monitored systems in the 2024 J.D. Power Home Security Satisfaction Study.

### **5\. Abode**, best for smart-home enthusiasts

Abode positions its platform as both a security system and a smart-home hub. It supports integrations with HomeKit, Alexa, Google Home, Zigbee, and Z-Wave, allowing homeowners to connect locks, lights, thermostats, and other devices.

Because of this broad compatibility, Abode is often considered by homeowners who want their security system to work seamlessly with a wider smart-home ecosystem.

**Best for:** Homeowners who want advanced automation and broad smart-home compatibility.

**Reputation:** Abode’s BBB profile lists an A- rating.

Brinks Home offers a traditional security-company experience. Systems are often customized by experts and paired with professional monitoring.

Frontpoint offers DIY equipment paired with professional monitoring services. The platform supports smart-home devices and customizable equipment packages.

### **8\. Cove**, best for affordable monitored security

Cove focuses on simplicity and affordability. The company offers straightforward equipment packages and professional monitoring without long-term contracts.

The system typically includes basic intrusion sensors, motion detectors, and cameras that can be installed quickly. Because Cove emphasizes simple pricing and flexible plans, it is often considered by homeowners who want monitoring without complex service agreements.

**Best for:** Homeowners who want professional monitoring at a lower monthly cost.

**Reputation:** Cove systems frequently appear in expert security system comparisons for affordability and ease of use.

### **9\. Deep Sentinel**, best for AI-powered camera monitoring

Deep Sentinel takes a different approach to home security compared with traditional alarm systems. Its system focuses on AI-powered cameras paired with live security guards who can intervene through two-way audio when suspicious activity is detected.

Instead of relying primarily on door sensors and alarms, the system uses artificial intelligence to detect potential threats and then routes alerts to a live monitoring team. This approach is designed to deter intruders before they attempt to enter a home.

**Best for:** Homeowners who want advanced camera monitoring and active deterrence.

**Reputation:** Deep Sentinel is frequently highlighted for its AI-driven monitoring model and innovative approach to home security.
