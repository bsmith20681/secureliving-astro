

If you’re already a Vivint customer and you want to change providers (or you’re considering buying Vivint but want to look at other options first) then you’re going to want to consider either ADT or Cove.

If you want every feature that Vivint has (including home automation and security cameras) from a different provider, ADT is the way to go. But if the high price tag of Vivint is the reason you’re looking for alternatives, and don’t care about home automation, then consider Cove.

What To Look For In A Vivint Alternative

I based my recommendations around three important features that you should be looking for in a Vivint alternative.

  * Core Home Security Monitoring: You want something with a cellular connection, 24/7 monitoring to police, fire, and medical, and built in two-way voice in the keypad
  * Outside Security Cameras: High quality cameras that will send you alerts and notifications, record video that you can review in the app on your phone
  * Smart Home: Something that lets you control smart home devices (thermostats, door locks, lights, etc) remotely from your phone

ADT: A Near One For One Alternative

ADT has the closest feature set to Vivint out of any security system. If you want to make the switch to a different provider but still want home automation and security cameras, this is the system to get.

Monitoring and the ADT Command Panel

Like Vivint, ADT systems are monitored through a cellular connection (not through your phone line or Wi-Fi) which is a big plus.

They come with a touchscreen Command Panel that has two-way talk built in (just like the Vivint Smart Hub panel). This means that the monitoring rep can talk to you through your phone or your ADT panel. If a break-in is in progress and you’re inside the home, they’ll be able to communicate with you even if you can’t answer your phone.

The Google Nest Cameras

The ADT cameras are Google Nest cameras. They’re very high quality, with great video, night vision, and they stream right to your phone. You don’t get the deter mode (like you do with Vivint cameras) but they still do a great job.

One of the big advantages to the ADT cameras is the fact that they’re Google Nest cameras. If you ever cancel your ADT subscription, you’ll still be able to use the cameras through the Google Home app. With Vivint, you lose access to the cameras in the app if you cancel your subscription.

Also, you don’t pay an additional fee for each camera like you do with Vivint. You pay a flat rate of $48.99 per month whether you have one or ten cameras.

Smart Home Automation

ADT is compatible with any Z-Wave devices, just like Vivint. You can add smart door locks, thermostats, garage door controllers, smart light switches, etc. You’ll be able to control them all from the ADT app, and you can even create “automations” (like when the system is armed at night, the garage door closes and the lights turn off).

The ADT automation features are not as detailed as what you get with Vivint, but it’s good enough to handle the most popular automations.

Pricing and Contract

You’ll pay $42.99 a month for the basic security system. Add cameras and the price goes up to $48.99 per month.

Equipment starts at $369 and you can finance it over 36 months. When I recorded my video, ADT was running a promotion for a free doorbell camera and a $100 Visa gift card.

Cove: A More Affordable Option

You can get all the core home security features of Vivint from Cove at a much more affordable price.

Monitoring and the Cove Touchscreen Keypad

Like Vivint, Cove systems have a touchscreen panel with two-way voice built in. Cove systems use your home Wi-Fi connection but have a cellular backup (if your power or internet goes out).

Cove’s Security Cameras

Cove cameras have HD video, night vision, and two-way talk. You don’t get the deter mode that Vivint offers, but overall I was impressed with the video quality and performance.

The outdoor camera records to an SD card and is powered by a solar panel. That means if your power or Wi-Fi goes down the camera will continue to record. (I live in Central Florida so I didn’t have any problems with the solar panel keeping the camera charged. But if you live in a part of the country that doesn’t get a lot of sun you might want to think about that).

DIY Installation

Unlike Vivint, Cove is a Do It Yourself system. You’ll need to install it yourself. It took me about three hours to install the entire system.

The thing you sacrifice with Cove is home automation. There’s no way to control your smart locks or thermostats through the Cove system.

Cove Security Pricing

Standard security monitoring costs $23 a month. If you add cameras, monitoring costs $33 per month. But $5 of that $33 goes into a credit on your account that you can use to purchase additional equipment.

So your true cost is only $28 a month if you have cameras. When I recorded my video, Cove was running a promotion for 70% off equipment plus a free indoor camera. I paid $250 for my system, which included three cameras and all the sensors you see in the picture.

Other Home Security Systems We Considered

Ring and SimpliSafe are popular home security systems, but we didn’t include them in this comparison because they don’t have two-way voice in their control panel and the cameras don’t record if you don’t have Wi-Fi.

Which Should You Choose?

If the price of Vivint is the problem, and you want to continue to have a security system but spend less money, get Cove.

If you want all the features that you have with Vivint, including home automation, from a different provider, get ADT.
