* * *

If the premium feature set feels like more than you need, the pricing gives you pause, or you’d rather skip a long-term contract, there are solid alternatives worth a serious look.

Out of the dozens of systems we’ve tested, ADT, SimpliSafe, and Cove consistently stood out as the strongest Vivint alternatives. Here’s how each one stacks up directly against our Vivint experience, so you have a clear, apples-to-apples comparison for deciding.

    #### ADT as a Vivint Alternative
    
    Vivint and ADT represent different priorities. Vivint leans into cutting-edge equipment and smart home integration, while ADT’s strength is reliability built over more than 150 years in business. That track record is hard to match. ADT backs it up with serious monitoring infrastructure as well, operating six redundant monitoring centers for reliability compared to Vivint’s two.
    
    **Pro Tip:** The more monitoring centers, the better. If a natural disaster or power outage impacts one region, ADT’s other monitoring centers are sure to keep its customers online and secure. 
    
    #### Equipment
    
    ADT’s equipment has historically trailed Vivint’s, but the gap is narrowing. We did miss Vivint’s Smart Deter feature on the Outdoor Camera Pro, which uses LED lights and audible alarms to flag suspicious activity before it escalates. It kept neighborhood kids from toilet-papering our house on Halloween, which counts for something.
    
    That said, ADT impressed us in areas where Vivint falls short. The facial recognition on the Nest Cams delivered more intelligent alerts than anything we got from Vivint. When a friend stopped by, we got a notification identifying her by the name we set in the app rather than a generic “person detected” alert. ADT prices its Nest Cam lineup to match what you’d pay buying directly through Google. Here’s a quick overview.

    #### Monitoring Plans
    
    ADT and Vivint have brought their monitoring costs closer in line over time. At the premium tier, our [ADT monitoring plan](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/adt/) with cameras came to $49.99 per month, which also bundles Google Home Premium for the Nest cameras included with ADT systems. A comparable Vivint setup ran us $44.99 per month for its top-tier plan, about $5 less than ADT, though both systems land in similar territory at the higher end.

    #### SimpliSafe as a Vivint Alternative
    
    If free DIY monitoring is a priority, SimpliSafe is worth a close look. The equipment holds up well, professional installation is available if you want it, and self-installation is straightforward and free. What sets SimpliSafe apart from Vivint is the degree of control it hands to the user. Rather than steering you toward a particular setup, it gives you the flexibility to run your system on your own terms.
    
    #### Equipment
    
    Our SimpliSafe testing left us genuinely impressed, though the equipment doesn’t quite match Vivint’s premium feel. The analog keypad is the most obvious example — it’s reliable, but it lacks the touchscreen functionality we appreciated on Vivint, including the ability to pull up outdoor camera livestreams directly from the panel.
    
    SimpliSafe also doesn’t offer a battery-powered video doorbell. Instead, we had to replace our existing doorbell during installation. Worth noting that Vivint doesn’t offer one either. Once installed, the doorbell performed well — our [SimpliSafe Video Doorbell Series 2 review](https://www.security.org/doorbell-camera/simplisafe/review/) covers the full experience.
    
    **FYI:** It may seem obvious, but wired video doorbells are harder for burglars to steal than wireless options. They also get less interference that can interrupt their Wi-Fi signals. That makes hardwired options more robust and reliable. We can still get annoyed about the longer installation process though.
    
    On the plus side, [SimpliSafe’s pricing](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/simplisafe/) reflects its more basic equipment. Our Vivint system with three sensors, an outdoor camera, a smart lock, and a smoke and carbon monoxide detector cost nearly $1,200. A comparable system from SimpliSafe costs less than $650. That’s some big savings.
    
    #### Monitoring Plans
    
    SimpliSafe offers several monitoring tiers, but we recommend skipping the lowest tier. The mid-tier Core plan ($32.99 per month) and above include Intruder Intervention and 30 days of cloud storage — two features you won’t find in the entry-level option. Intruder Intervention lets live monitoring agents see and speak through your indoor cameras during an active alarm, so they can assess the situation in real time and even address an intruder directly, which can be enough to make them flee before authorities arrive.
    
    The Pro and Pro Plus plans ($49.99 and $79.99 per month) go even further with Active Guard outdoor protection. When outdoor cameras detect lurkers or intruders approaching the house — using AI to distinguish them from normal foot traffic — live monitors can intervene through two-way talk before someone even reaches the door.

If budget is a priority and you have the capability to self-monitor, this is a great option.

    When price is a big factor, you can’t go wrong with Cove. It covers all the basics you need to secure your home, plus a few nice additions. Every package is fully customized, ensuring you never end up with equipment you don’t need.
    
    Vivint fully customizes its systems, too, but you need to talk to a Vivint salesperson to get your system. That means you need to tread the water of upsells to avoid anything unnecessary. Cove lets you buy your entire system online so you can take your time picking out your ideal system without any pressure.
    
    **Pro Tip:** We recommend one entry sensor on every ground-floor door and window, one outdoor camera, one video doorbell, and one indoor camera for most homes. Putting together a list of equipment you need before calling a home security provider for help building out your system can help you avoid pesky upsells.
    
    #### Equipment

Cove’s doorbell camera, priced under $100, also outperformed our expectations during live testing.

## Which One Is Right for You?

We tested dozens of home security systems to find which systems can stand-in for Vivint.

Vivint is a great home security system. If you want smart-home capabilities and cutting-edge technology, it’s hard to beat. Some people prioritize reliability above all else. For them, we like ADT. It’s one of our favorite security systems overall, and it gets better year after year.

Others want complete control over their security system. In those cases, we recommend SimpliSafe. You don’t get locked into anything, and you can fully self-monitor your security system if you want. If your budget is the main concern, Cove gets the job done. There aren’t nearly as many bells and whistles, but the equipment costs a fraction of what Vivint charges.

## FAQs About Vivint Alternatives

-   [What’s the best Vivint alternative?](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/vivint/alternatives/#)
    
    The best Vivint alternative depends on why you’re looking for a Vivint alternative. We like ADT for anyone who wants a company with a long-standing history, SimpliSafe if you don’t want to be locked into monitoring, and Cove when Vivint can’t fit into your budget.
    
-   [Is Vivint a good security system?](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/vivint/alternatives/#)
    
    Yes, Vivint is one of our favorite security systems. It features cutting-edge equipment, high-quality monitoring, and white-glove installation.
    
-   [How much more expensive is Vivint?](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/vivint/alternatives/#)
    
    Most of Vivint’s security system is about double the price of the industry norm. In our opinion, Vivint justifies that price increase with its premium-quality products.
    
-   [Can I get a Vivint system without a contract?](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/vivint/alternatives/#)
    
    As long as you’re willing to pay for your equipment upfront, you can get a Vivint system without a contract. HomeProtect starter packages begin at $349.99, plus a 199installationfee(199.99 for equipment and free installation if you sign a three-year contract), but most users will want a more complete setup to unlock Vivint’s home automation capabilities. Full systems can easily run $1,000 or more. Keep in mind that even without a financing contract, you’ll still need a professional monitoring plan to access most features.
    
-   [How do I cancel my Vivint contract?](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/vivint/alternatives/#)
    
    Unfortunately, there’s not a whole lot you can do to get out of your Vivint contract once you pass the money-back-guarantee timeframe.
