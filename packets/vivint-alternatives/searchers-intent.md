# Searcher's Intent: Vivint Alternatives

## Who is searching

Someone who probably has Vivint already. They want to keep having home security, just not with Vivint.

## Why they're leaving Vivint

1. **The price is too high.** Talk about more affordable systems and what they cost per month compared to Vivint, Cove would be the better option here since it still has the same core security features as vivint just at a lower price espcially for the equipment. However, you do sacrifice the home automation part. However, if they want all the same features including home automation then ADT would be a better option. Espcially if they want cameras. With Vivint, you have to pay an extra $5 per month per camera where as with ADT you don't. So you could have 1 or 6 cameras and the monthly stays the same. 
2. **They're unhappy with Vivint's customer service.** They want the same feature set from a different provider.
3. **They're unhappy with the equipment quality.** They want the same feature set with better equipment from a different provider.

## What the article should do

Address those reasons directly. A reader leaving over price should come away knowing the cheaper option, and a reader leaving over service or equipment should come away knowing which provider gives them what they have with ADT now, without the problem they're leaving.
