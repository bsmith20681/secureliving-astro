# Best SimpliSafe Alternative

## **Top SimpliSafe Alternatives**

SimpliSafe has become a popular starting point for homeowners who want a straightforward, affordable security system. Its DIY setup, simple equipment packages, and flexible monitoring plans make it easy to get up and running quickly.

But as needs evolve, some homeowners begin looking for alternatives.

In many cases, the decision is less about replacing SimpliSafe and more about expanding beyond its core strengths. The key question becomes: do you want to keep things simple, or move toward a system with more capability, customization, or support?

Some platforms prioritize ease of use and minimal setup. Others offer deeper integrations, broader protection, or more hands-on service over time.

This guide explores leading alternatives to SimpliSafe and how they compare across setup, monitoring, smart-home capabilities, and long-term flexibility.

## **When It Makes Sense to Look Beyond SimpliSafe**

SimpliSafe works well for straightforward home security setups. However, certain needs can push homeowners to consider other options:

-   You want more advanced smart-home integrations or automation
-   You prefer professional installation instead of self-setup
-   You need expanded monitoring (fire, CO, water, etc.)
-   You want ongoing local service and system support
-   Your home or security needs have become more complex over time
-   You prefer live monitoring instead of relying on app notifications alone

In these situations, alternative systems may provide additional flexibility or support.

### **3\. Ring – DIY system with a stronger focus on video**

Ring is often used by homeowners who want to expand a camera-based setup into a broader security system.

The platform includes video doorbells, indoor and outdoor cameras, alarm systems, and sensors, all managed through a single app. Optional professional monitoring is available, and the system integrates with Amazon Alexa devices.

Where SimpliSafe centers more on intrusion detection, Ring places greater emphasis on video visibility, alerts, and recorded activity.

**Best for:** Homeowners who want to build around cameras and visual monitoring.

### **4\. Abode – Greater flexibility for smart-home integration**

Abode is designed for users who want more control over how their security system connects with other smart-home devices.

It supports integrations with Apple HomeKit, Google Home, Alexa, Zigbee, and Z-Wave, allowing users to connect locks, lighting, thermostats, and other devices within a single system.

Compared to SimpliSafe, Abode generally offers more flexibility and automation options, though it may require more time to configure and manage.

**Best for:** Homeowners who want deeper smart-home integration and customization.

### **5\. Frontpoint – DIY system with more guided support**

Frontpoint maintains a DIY installation model but adds more support throughout setup and ongoing use.

Homeowners can choose from a range of sensors, cameras, and smart-home devices, with systems designed to be easy to install while still allowing customization. Professional monitoring is available with mobile app control.

Relative to SimpliSafe, Frontpoint places more emphasis on customer support and system guidance, while still keeping the DIY structure.

**Best for:** Homeowners who want a DIY system with additional support.

### **6\. Cove – Simplified system with predictable pricing**

Cove focuses on offering a straightforward security system with quick setup and simple pricing.

The system includes core components such as entry sensors, motion detectors, and a central hub, with optional professional monitoring and no long-term contracts. Setup is designed to be completed in a short amount of time.

Compared to SimpliSafe, Cove offers a similar level of simplicity, often with fewer configuration and customization options.

**Best for:** Homeowners who want a simple, low-commitment system.

### **7\. Brinks Home – Hybrid model with added structure**

Brinks Home offers a mix of DIY flexibility and professional support.

Homeowners may install the system themselves, but equipment selection and configuration can involve guidance from the provider. Systems typically include sensors, cameras, and smart-home integrations, along with professional monitoring.

Compared to SimpliSafe, Brinks Home introduces more structure and provider involvement while still allowing some flexibility in setup.

**Best for:** Homeowners who want a balance between independence and support
