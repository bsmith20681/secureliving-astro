# Searcher's Intent: SimpliSafe Alternatives

## Who is searching

Someone who probably has SimpliSafe already. They want to keep having home security, just not with SimpliSafe.

## Why they're leaving SimpliSafe

1. **They want more features, but same price** The great thing about simplisafe is that they are affordable. They lack a lot of features though. So someone may want to go with something else in order to have better home automation features or better home security features. 

For example, Cove offers a touchscreen keypad and two way voice for the same price as SimpliSafe. Also the equipment is about half the price. Also if they cancel Cove they can continue to use the cameras on their own since they come from Eufy. 
2. **They're unhappy with SimpliSafe's customer service.** They want the same feature set from a different provider.
3. **They're unhappy with the equipment quality.** They want the same feature set with better equipment from a different provider. 

## What the article should do

Address those reasons directly. A reader leaving over price should come away knowing the cheaper option, and a reader leaving over service or equipment should come away knowing which provider gives them what they have with SimpliSafe now, without the problem they're leaving.
