# SimpliSafe Alternatives: Top Security Systems Compared

[SimpliSafe](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/simplisafe/) has earned its reputation as a solid DIY home security system, but it's not the right fit for everyone. Maybe you're frustrated with SimpliSafe's limited smart home integrations, or perhaps you're looking for more advanced equipment like touchscreens instead of keypads. Whatever your reason for exploring alternative [home security systems](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/), you're making a smart move by doing your research.

We still believe SimpliSafe is one of the [best home security systems](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/best/) on the market. SimpliSafe offers some compelling features, including Intruder Intervention for indoor cameras and Active Guard Outdoor Protection for outdoor use. However, SimpliSafe's competitors have been stepping up their game. In this article, we'll walk through three strong alternatives that might actually suit your needs better, whether that's installation ease, affordability, or intruder deterrence technology.

## 1\. ADT Blu: Best Alternative for Easy DIY Installation

ADT Blu brings the trusted ADT name to the DIY security market without requiring a professional technician, a long-term contract, or a credit check. During our testing, we found ADT Blu nailed the balance between professional-grade protection and user-friendly installation. The highlight is ADT's own 2K camera lineup — the ADT Blu Indoor Camera ($69.99), Outdoor Camera ($89.99), and Doorbell Camera ($109.99) — which integrate directly with the ADT+ app and support smart detection for people, animals, vehicles, and packages.

Another reason ADT Blu is a strong SimpliSafe alternative is its professional monitoring service. It's backed by the same award-winning ADT monitoring infrastructure as the professionally installed systems. ADT has six interconnected monitoring centers across North America to ensure someone is always available, even during natural disasters or regional outages.

**FYI:** ADT includes video verification in its camera-supported professional monitoring plans. Video-verified alarms can speed up police response.1

### Pros

-   No contract required, with month-to-month monitoring plans available
-   Professional installation option available if you prefer a hands-off setup
-   Six-month money-back guarantee offers a substantially longer trial period than competitors
-   Compatible with Z-Wave and Matter

### Cons

-   Higher monthly monitoring costs compared to budget alternatives
-   Limited self-monitoring features
-   Professional installation is only available with contracted systems
-   Video support only available on most expensive plan

### Installation Experience

It took us about 35 minutes to set up our ADT Blu system with five sensors and an ADT Blu Outdoor Camera. We used the ADT+ app whenever we got stuck, but it was also reassuring to know that we could watch video demonstrations on how to set up various ADT Blu components.

We found the adhesive backing on the sensors stronger than that of SimpliSafe's. The ADT Blu cameras are entirely wire-free by default, which makes placement straightforward — we tried three different locations for the Outdoor Camera before settling on the best angle without any mounting or wiring commitment.

### Monitoring and Response

ADT Blu's monitoring is flexible and more affordable than ADT's professionally installed systems. Self-monitoring starts at $9.99 per month for camera-only setups and $14.99 per month for full systems. Professional monitoring starts at $24.99 per month for systems without cameras and $34.99 per month for systems with cameras — that's competitive to SimpliSafe's Core plan at $32.99 a month while including ADT's full monitoring network behind it.

We still believe you’re getting a good bang for your buck with ADT. When we triggered test alarms, we received a text message instantly, and monitoring agents called within 30 seconds if there was no response. They asked us security questions to confirm we needed help before dispatching emergency services. We like that ADT doesn't just call the primary contact and give up. They'll work through your entire contact list to verify the emergency, reducing false alarm dispatches that can result in fines from local police departments.

## 2\. Cove: Best Alternative Affordable Option

Cove makes financial sense for homeowners who want reliable protection without premium pricing. Professional monitoring starts at $19.99 per month, but like ADT, you’ll need Cove’s most expensive plan for video support. It’s still cheaper than ADT and SimpliSafe at $29.99 a month.

We found the equipment straightforward but functional. [Motion detectors](https://www.safehome.org/motion-sensors/) are slightly bulkier than SimpliSafe's, but they're more affordable to expand your system over time. The touchscreen panel is modern and responsive, though it doesn’t feel as premium as our next pick, Vivint. For most people, that's a reasonable trade-off when you're saving $20 to $30 monthly on monitoring fees.

### Pros

-   Low monthly monitoring costs starting at $19.99
-   Lifetime equipment warranty on Cove+ eliminates long-term replacement costs
-   60-day money-back trial period, double the industry standard
-   Regularly runs sales throughout the year up to 70 percent off

### Cons

-   Video support only available with most expensive monitoring plan
-   Fewer third-party integrations than competitors
-   No self-monitoring option
-   Some of the equipment is bulkier than SimpliSafe

### Value Proposition

Every time we visit Cove’s website, they seem to be running a sale with discounts up to 70 percent off. We’ve bought [door sensors](https://www.safehome.org/motion-sensors/door/) for less than $5 and a flood sensor for just $37.50. The lifetime warranty included in the Cove+ plan means you won't face unexpected replacement costs if sensors fail after the standard warranty period expires. Plus, it’s easy to add new devices to your system over time if you move or need more coverage.

[Cove’s professional monitoring plans](https://www.safehome.org/security-systems/cove/) are just as affordable, starting at $19.99 per month. As we mentioned earlier, if you need video support, you’ll have to go with Cove+, which is $29.99 per month. We think it’s worth it, since it also includes voice support with Google Assistant and Alexa and a $5-per-month equipment reward. We used this to get some more entry sensors for our windows upstairs.

**Pro Tip:** We recommend taking advantage of Cove's 60-day trial by testing your system with deliberate false alarms. Let the monitoring center know first, so you don’t get any unnecessary visitors. It’s a great way to test that all sensors trigger properly and the monitoring response meets your expectations.

## 3\. Vivint: Best Alternative Proactive Deterrence

Like SimpliSafe, Vivint focuses on prevention rather than just reaction. Vivint’s Smart Deter technology uses artificial intelligence to analyze activity around your property and actively deter potential intruders before they attempt entry. While ADT and Cove sound alarms after someone breaks in, Vivint aims to stop break-ins from happening in the first place.

Professional installation is required, and we’re glad it is. Technicians assess your home's vulnerabilities, recommend optimal sensor placement, and ensure everything connects properly before leaving. Our technician spent about 4 hours installing devices and answering our questions throughout the process. For anyone who values expertise over DIY installation, this approach fully addresses installation concerns.

### Pros

### Cons

-   Expensive equipment costs
-   Contracts typically required, though month-to-month options exist at higher prices

-   Camera support only available on $50 monitoring plan

### Smart Deter Technology

![Vivint Outdoor Camera Pro - Closeup](https://www.safehome.org/app/uploads/2020/09/Vivint-Outdoor-Camera-Closeup.jpg "Vivint Outdoor Camera Pro – Closeup")

Vivint’s Outdoor Camera looks tiny, but it packs a lot of punch with its deterrence features.

We found Vivint's Smart Deter option more advanced than SimpliSafe’s Active Guard Outdoor Protection service. Outdoor cameras use AI to distinguish between people, vehicles, and animals. When the system detects a person loitering near entry points or approaching windows during unusual hours, it automatically triggers bright LED lights, sound deterrent tones, and warning lights that you can customize. Monitoring agents can also intervene through two-way audio to verbally warn potential intruders that they're being recorded and that police will be contacted.

Research shows that visible security cameras and alarms can deter criminals by up to 46 percent.2 Vivint’s Outdoor Cameras’ deterrence features ensure that if they decide to go through with it, they change their mind quickly.

**Pro Tip:** We recommend using Vivint outdoor cameras to monitor approach paths rather than just entry points. This way, you’ll catch potential intruders before they reach doors or windows, maximizing Smart Deter's effectiveness.

### Smart Home Integration

Vivint's smart home ecosystem controls security, door locks, thermostats, lights, garage doors, and cameras through the Vivint app with unified automation. You can create scenes like “Goodnight” that arm security, lock doors, adjust the thermostat, and turn off lights with one command.

We found the integration works with Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa, but functionality is richer within Vivint's native ecosystem. The touchscreen panel became our smart home hub, displaying camera feeds, controlling devices, and managing security settings from a central interface. If you’re into smart home automation rather than basic security monitoring, Vivint's approach justifies the premium pricing.

## Is SimpliSafe Still Worth It?

SimpliSafe is a solid choice despite strong competition, particularly for renters or homeowners who prioritize simplicity and moderate pricing. Their Intruder Intervention and Active Guard Outdoor Protection features provide monitoring agent intervention capabilities that compete directly with Vivint's deterrence technology at lower price points. The Standard plan at $22.99 monthly offers basic professional monitoring, while the Pro Plus plan at $79.99 includes 24/7 Active Guard protection that actively deters intruders through two-way talk, sirens, and lights on outdoor cameras.

SimpliSafe’s equipment, starting at approximately $250, is significantly cheaper than Vivint’s while offering more features than Cove’s budget packages. We installed our SimpliSafe system in 30 minutes without any issues. However, limited smart home integrations and less sophisticated automation may restrict tech-savvy users compared with higher-end alternatives.

To keep our comparisons fair, we test all security systems in the same environment: a two-story, 2,200-square-foot house. We even install the devices in roughly the same locations. In our experience, testing security equipment in an actual home setup produces the most accurate results.

## Verdict: Should You Stick With SimpliSafe?

SimpliSafe remains competitive with its Intruder Intervention and Active Guard features, providing monitoring agent support at moderate price points that fall between budget and premium options. Choosing the right SimpliSafe alternative depends on your specific priorities and budget constraints.

We believe ADT Blu delivers the easiest DIY installation experience backed by industry-leading monitoring infrastructure. Cove offers the most affordable long-term ownership costs with lifetime equipment warranties that eliminate replacement expenses. Vivint has the most sophisticated proactive deterrence technology with comprehensive smart home integration.

Your choice ultimately depends on whether you prioritize installation simplicity, affordability, advanced deterrence technology, or balanced features at mid-range pricing. Keep in mind that home security is too important to choose based solely on marketing materials. ## Frequently Asked Questions

-   [Can I install these systems myself, or do I need professional installation?](https://www.safehome.org/compare/simplisafe-alternative/#)
    
    Cove does not offer a self-monitoring option. All Cove systems require a professional monitoring plan. That said, Cove’s plans are relatively affordable. Cove Basic delivers 24/7 monitoring, alarm verification, and complete control through the Cove app for $22.99 per month — competitive with other budget-friendly plans like SimpliSafe's Standard plan.
    
-   [What happens to my security system if my internet goes out?](https://www.safehome.org/compare/simplisafe-alternative/#)
    
    Systems with cellular backup continue monitoring through cellular connection if internet fails. Keep in mind that some features, like camera streaming, typically require internet connectivity to function.
    
-   [How long do security system batteries typically last?](https://www.safehome.org/compare/simplisafe-alternative/#)
    
    In our experience, sensor batteries last between three and five years depending on usage frequency, while camera batteries can work for up to 12 months before needing a recharge. Most systems alert you 30 days before batteries need a recharge or replacement.
    
-   [Can I take my security system with me if I move?](https://www.safehome.org/compare/simplisafe-alternative/#)
    
    Yes, all DIY systems discussed are fully portable. Simply remove sensors and reinstall at your new home. Vivint equipment is also removable, but professional reinstallation may be required.
