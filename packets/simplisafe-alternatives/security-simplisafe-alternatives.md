# The Best SimpliSafe Alternatives of 2026

ADT scores 9.8/10 for best professional monitoring, Vivint excels at smart home integration with 9.4/10, and Cove is most affordable at 8.7/10.

We like our SimpliSafe security system for plenty of reasons – reasons that we all laid out in our [SimpliSafe home security review](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/simplisafe/review/). It’s easily  one of the [best security systems around](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/best/). But we get it; it’s not for everybody. You might want top-tier professional installation. Or, the most advanced smart home integrations to make their home feel like it could be in The Jetsons. And even though we’d consider SimpliSafe affordable, it’s far from the least expensive security system around.

So, we put together a list of the best SimpliSafe alternatives based on our testing of over 60 security systems. We looked for security systems that excel at SimpliSafe’s shortcomings. By doing that, we decided the top three alternatives to SimpliSafe are ADT, Vivint, and Cove.

ADT, Vivint, and Cove each beat SimpliSafe in a different way: monitoring, smart home tech, or price.

That’s because ADT offers a few more capabilities than SimpliSafe on top of a longer-standing reputation without giving up much. The only real drawback to ADT is the slightly higher price point.
    
    ### ADT’s Installation and Monitoring
    
    ![ADT Home Security Yard Sign](https://www.security.org/app/uploads/2022/01/Our-ADT-Home-Security-Yard-Sign.jpg "ADT Home Security Yard Sign")
    
    We proudly displayed our ADT yard sign so burglars knew our home was protected.
    
    ADT has been around for more than 140 years,1 so unlike SimpliSafe, it takes a more old-school approach. What we mean by that is that it prioritizes professional installation and monitoring. ADT used to require both, and its DIY option is now available separately through ADT Blu.
    
    Here’s the thing though: Because ADT customers will be more likely to sign up for professional monitoring, it’s a more secure option. With monitoring, ADT will dispatch the police to your house in case you need help. That means you’re never on your own. Plus, ADT offers the most robust monitoring service in the industry with 12 redundant monitoring centers. With SimpliSafe, professional monitoring is optional with free and paid self-monitoring plans as well.
    
    [ADT’s monitoring plans](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/adt/) offer comparable price points to SimpliSafe. ADT typically runs $49.99 per month for full coverage, including cameras, while ADT Blu, its DIY platform, runs from $24.99 to $34.99 depending on whether or not you have cameras. [SimpliSafe plans](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/simplisafe/) range from $22.99 to $79.99. We recommend most people go with SimpliSafe’s $32.99 per month plan whereas ADT requires a specific plan depending on your security equipment.
    
    ### ADT’s Smart Home Capabilities
    
    ![Our Nest Learning Thermostat synced up seamlessly with ADT.](https://www.security.org/app/uploads/2024/09/vivint-nest-thermostat-2.jpg "Our Nest Learning Thermostat synced up seamlessly with ADT.")
    
    Our Nest Learning Thermostat synced up seamlessly with ADT.
    
    Despite its long history, ADT is on the cutting edge when it comes to technology. The ADT+ app is one of the [most intuitive, user-friendly home security mobile apps](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/best/smartphone-app/) out there. It’s what we used to integrate our system with our Alexa and Google Home ecosystems.
    
    Integrating with Alexa or Google Home let us activate and deactivate our system with our voices, otherwise known as voice control. We will say the Google integration gave us more capabilities and it was easier to set up since ADT is partnered with Google. They even offer Google Nest Cams now. ADT’s smart home integration went beyond Alexa and Google Home though and included [smart lights](https://www.security.org/smart-home/smart-lights/costs/), [smart locks](https://www.security.org/smart-locks/), and smart thermostats for a complete [home automation](https://www.security.org/smart-home/) set up.
    
    **Expert Insight:** Speaking of automation, ADT’s best automation feature right now is Trusted Neighbor. Using the facial recognition on the Nest Cams, your ADT system can automatically unlock the door (provided you have a smart lock) and disarm the system to let a trusted contact in during emergencies or at certain times of the day to run errands for you.
    
    ### ADT’s Pricing
    
    If we have a complaint about ADT, it’s the higher price point compared to SimpliSafe. Although, ADT has come closer to SimpliSafe’s pricing in recent years. For comparison, ADT’s starter package retails at $269 while SimpliSafe’s retails at $250. And as we added more components to our system, the price gap got bigger. ADT offers financing options to spread out the cost of our system over three years. SimpliSafe only offers up to two-year financing.
    
    Still, you can’t put a price on trust, and with almost a century and a half of a solid reputation, we knew we could trust ADT to take care of our homes.

They require professional installation and monitoring, so if you’re not ready to commit to a monthly fee, you should look elsewhere for [self-monitored home security](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/best/no-monthly-fee/).

Vivint may be a little more expensive than some other companies, both in terms of equipment and monitoring, but it’s certainly not overpriced, given just how good a system it is.
    
    ### Vivint’s Smart Home Capabilities
    
    ![Vivint Smart Lock](https://www.security.org/app/uploads/2024/07/vivint-smart-lock-1.jpg "Vivint Smart Lock")

    We mentioned Vivint’s advanced technology earlier and its smart home capabilities is one place where those capabilities shine. While it can pair with smart home ecosystems like Alexa and Google Home, it goes further than that. Instead of pairing Vivint to our Echo Show, we paired our smart home devices to our Vivint system. All of it could be controlled from our Vivint touchscreen control panel or the user-friendly mobile app.
    
    Since we paired our living room lights with our Vivint system, we made our Vivint Outdoor Camera Pro’s active deterrence even more powerful. Whenever it detects a lurker, which turns on its LED light, we also have it turn on our living room light. That can make anyone thinking about breaking into our home think that the house is occupied.
    
    **FYI:** The Vivint Outdoor Camera Pro features High-dynamic range (HDR) resolution which balances out the dark and light areas of an image, providing more gradations between the two. This helps eliminate shadow and glare.
    
    ### Vivint’s Pricing
    
    Vivint has taken several steps to become more affordable. While the Outdoor Camera Pro we mentioned cost us $399.99, Vivint recently introduced the HomeProtect system, which starts at just $199.99. That’s one of the most affordable packages we’ve seen from Vivint. A complete HomeProtect Pro system starts at $599.99 with professional installation included.
    
    As we mentioned earlier, Vivint requires a contract and professional monitoring, which starts at $24.99 per month. Now, a contract is only required for systems with financed equipment, but because of the price of Vivint hardware, most customers choose to finance. Unlike ADT contracts, which are typically three years long, Vivint contracts can be up to five years. While price used to be Vivint’s biggest downside compared to SimpliSafe, the new HomeProtect options have made them much more competitive.

Cove Plus adds camera access and cloud storage, while Cove Premium extends the cloud storage, and adds AI-powered alerts and a lifetime equipment warranty.

## Recap

When it comes to top-tier security systems, you have choices. SimpliSafe is a great system, and we recommend it highly. But it’s not for everyone as it does have a few minor drawbacks.

For instance, if you want the best professional installation and monitoring, we’d recommend ADT instead of SimpliSafe. On the other hand, for anyone looking for top-tier smart home capabilities, Vivint blows SimpliSafe out of the water. And if a SimpliSafe system is too expensive for you, Cove can fit almost any budget with systems. ## FAQs About SimpliSafe Alternatives

-   [What is the best alternative to SimpliSafe?](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/simplisafe/alternatives/#)
    
    The best SimpliSafe alternative depends on the reason you’re looking for an alternative to SimpliSafe. If you want better professional installation and monitoring, we recommend ADT. For better smart home capabilities, Vivint offers some of the most advanced smart features available. To save some money, Cove offers extremely affordable security systems.
    
-   [Is SimpliSafe a good security system?](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/simplisafe/alternatives/#)
    
    Yes, we think SimpliSafe is one of the best security systems available. But, it’s not the best option for everyone. We recommend SimpliSafe for anyone looking for a DIY security system with plenty of flexibility.
    
-   [Are there cheaper security systems than SimpliSafe?](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/simplisafe/alternatives/#)
    
    Although SimpliSafe offers some of the best value in the industry, Cove’s prices are lower across the board.
    
-   [Does SimpliSafe offer professional installation?](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/simplisafe/alternatives/#)
    
    While you can get your SimpliSafe system professionally installed, it’s not our favorite professional installation service. Instead of installing the system themselves, they hire a third-party to install your system. We preferred the in-house professional installation we received from ADT and Vivint.
    
-   [Can SimpliSafe integrate with my smart home ecosystem?](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/simplisafe/alternatives/#)
    
    SimpliSafe can work with both Amazon and Google Home smart home ecosystems. But, they don’t offer much in terms of controls. Basically, they just allow you to control your SimpliSafe system with your voice. More advanced systems like Vivint let us set up advanced automation so our security system could react to different scenarios automatically.
