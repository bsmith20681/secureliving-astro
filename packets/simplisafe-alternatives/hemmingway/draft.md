

If you’re looking for a new home security system to replace your current SimpliSafe system, then you’ve come to the right place. I have personally tested SimpliSafe, Cove, Ring, Vivint and ADT in my own house, and can tell you that there are some great alternatives to choose from.

The short answer is that for most people, Cove is the best alternative. But if you already have Ring cameras then Ring is a great option, and if you want to upgrade to professional installation and a more in-depth smart home system then Vivint is the way to go.

## What To Look For In A SimpliSafe Alternative

When choosing an alternative to SimpliSafe I focus on three main features.

 1. Affordable Pricing (both monthly and upfront)
 2. Month To Month Service With No Contract
 3. Solid Core Security Features (mobile app, cameras, etc…)

## SimpliSafe Alternatives Compared

## Why Cove Is The Best Alternative To SimpliSafe

Cove offers the same feature set as SimpliSafe at a lower price.

### The Touchscreen Keypad And Two-Way Voice

Where Cove is different is the type of keypad you get. SimpliSafe and Ring offer a traditional push button style keypad, where as Cove gives you a full color touchscreen that I find much easier to use.

Cove’s keypad also has two-way voice built in. This allows the monitoring station to talk directly to you if there is an alarm, and can be a huge benefit if you are home during a break-in.

### Professional Monitoring With Cellular Backup

Just like SimpliSafe, Cove’s professional monitoring connects to your home Wi-Fi network but also has a cellular backup connection in case your Wi-Fi or power goes out.

### Cameras That Record Without Wi-Fi

Cove uses Eufy branded cameras that have an SD card slot built right in to the camera. This means that even if your Wi-Fi goes down, your cameras continue to record. You can also keep using the cameras if you decide to cancel your service with Cove. And you can add as many cameras as you want without it costing you extra each month.

### DIY Installation

Installation of Cove is a breeze. It took me about 3 hours to install my system. You just stick the door and window sensors on with the adhesive strips (no tools required). The only part of the process I found frustrating was connecting the cameras to the system.

### Cove Pricing And Plans

Cove monitoring plans start at $23 per month. When you add cameras, the price goes up to $33 per month (the same as SimpliSafe).  But here’s the catch, $5 of that $33 per month accumulates as a credit on your account that you can use to purchase more equipment. So your true monthly cost for Cove is about $28.

When I recorded my video review of Cove, they were running a 70% off sale with a free indoor camera. I paid about $250 for my system (after the discount) and that included 3 cameras!

## Ring Is The Pick If You Already Own Ring Cameras

### Monitoring And The Keypad

Ring’s professional monitoring also uses your Wi-Fi network with a cellular backup connection. You get a basic push button style keypad which is okay, but I prefer the touchscreen that you get with Cove.

### Cameras And 24/7 Recording

Ring cameras are excellent, they are easy to install and have great video quality. What I like most is that you can pay $3 per month per camera for 24/7 recording (not just clips when motion is detected).

### Smart Home Devices And Alexa

Ring also supports some smart home devices such as door locks and thermostats. This is something that neither SimpliSafe or Cove can do.

### Ring Pricing

Ring’s equipment is more expensive than Cove, but if you already own Ring cameras and want to add an alarm system to your home this is a great option. Monitoring plans start at just $20 per month.

## Vivint Smart Home: Honorable Mention

If you buy all of Vivint’s equipment upfront (no financing) then you can have a month to month agreement like you can with SimpliSafe.

But the reason I didn’t include it in the list above is because it costs more.  Both the equipment and monthly service cost more than the other brands. If that’s not a concern for you then by all means check out Vivint, it is an excellent system.

### Where Does ADT Fit In?

ADT is the other professionally installed system I have reviewed. But it has a minimum 12 month agreement so doesn’t meet my criteria of being month to month.

## When Is SimpliSafe Worth Keeping?

There is one feature of SimpliSafe that none of the above systems offer, and that is live video monitoring. Their “Active Guard” service means that a live agent is watching your cameras and can alert the police if they see someone breaking in to your house.

## Which SimpliSafe Alternative Should You Choose?

As you can see there are several great alternatives to SimpliSafe.

If you want a similar system at a lower price with better equipment, go with Cove.

If you already have Ring cameras and want to add an alarm system to your home, Ring is a great choice.

And if you want to upgrade to professional installation and a much more in depth smart home system, then Vivint is worth the extra cost.
