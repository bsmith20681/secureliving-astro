

If you’re looking for alternatives to ADT, you’re in the right place. I’ve had ADT and all the other major security companies (SimpliSafe, Vivint, Ring, etc) in my own home so I have first-hand experience with each one.

There are a couple of great alternatives to ADT. My #1 pick is the Vivint system because it has all the same features as ADT, plus a few extras. If you want something that’s a lot more affordable, the Cove system is a great option.

## My Top Picks

* Get Vivint if you want the same features as ADT, plus more
* Get Cove if you want something that’s more affordable and doesn’t skimp on the core security features
* Don’t get SimpliSafe or Ring because they don’t have two-way talk, and the cameras don’t record without Wi-Fi

## What To Look For In A Good ADT Alternative

If you’re looking for a good replacement for ADT, here are a few key features that you’re going to want.

1. Security system that is monitored through a cellular connection.
2. Security system with two-way voice through the keypad.
3. Security cameras that you can access remotely (see live video, watch recorded video).
4. A system that has smart home capabilities.

## Vivint: The Closest Match To ADT

The Vivint system is probably the closest match to the ADT system because it has all the same features, plus a few more.

### Superb Monitoring Service And Two-Way Voice

First off, the Vivint system offers 24/7 monitoring to police, fire, and medical. They have a nice touchscreen keypad (just like the ADT system), and the system works through a cellular connection, meaning it doesn’t matter if your power or Wi-Fi goes out, your house will still be protected.

They also have two-way talk built inside of the keypad. So if you’re in the house when a break-in occurs, you won’t be in a position to pick up and answer your phone. But the Vivint agent can talk to you through the alarm panel and get the information they need to dispatch to police quickly.

### Best-In-Class Security Cameras With “Deter” Mode

The Vivint cameras are some of the best security cameras I’ve tested. Not only is the video quality excellent, they have a cool feature called “Deter Mode”.

If someone walks in front of a Vivint camera, you’ll get an alert on your phone (just like the ADT cameras), but the Vivint camera will also whistle at the person, and an LED light around the lens will start to flash. If you have the floodlight attachment, it will also light up like a floodlight.

If you’re a burglar and you know you’re being recorded, you’re probably not going to stick around for long!

Another great feature about the Vivint cameras is that they will record without Wi-Fi. All the major security companies (ADT, SimpliSafe, Ring, etc) will record in short video clips. Both ADT and Vivint have the option to upgrade to 24/7 continuous recording (you record every minute of every hour of every day).

The ADT and Vivint cameras will record video clips without Wi-Fi. The ADT cameras will only record up to one hour, whereas the Vivint cameras will record everything and send it to the panel, even if you don’t have Wi-Fi.

### Smart Home Automation Capabilities

If you want to control smart home devices like your lights, thermostats, or door locks, both the ADT and Vivint systems can connect to any Z-Wave device. So on paper, they appear to be the same.

But the way that the Vivint app is set up and how granular you can get with the smart home automations is far superior to the ADT system. It’s not until you have the app in your hand that you start to see the differences.

### Installation, Pricing, And Contract

The Vivint system is professionally installed (just like ADT). When I recorded my video review, Vivint was running a promotion for a free doorbell camera and free installation.

You have the option to purchase all the equipment up front and then the service is month-to-month (no contract). Or, you can finance the equipment (0% interest) over 42 or 60 months.

## Cove: The Most Affordable ADT Alternative

The Cove system is a little more affordable than the ADT system, and it doesn’t skimp on any of the core security features.

### Reliable Monitoring, Great Panel, And Good Cameras

Cove has a nice touchscreen panel (just like ADT and Vivint) that has two-way voice built in. The system is monitored 24/7 to police, fire, medical. The system connects to the monitoring center via your Wi-Fi network, but it has a cellular backup. So if your power and internet go out, you’ll still have monitoring.

The cameras are really nice too. You get all the same features that you get with the ADT and Vivint cameras (live view, two-way talk, recording). The Cove cameras will also record without Wi-Fi (they have a built-in SD card).

The only down side to the Cove cameras is that they only record video clips. There is no option to upgrade to 24/7 continuous recording like there is with ADT and Vivint.

### Installation And Pricing

Cove is a DIY system. It took me about three hours to install my system. When I recorded my video review, they had a promotion going on for 70% off all equipment and a free indoor camera. I paid about $250 up front for my system.

The Cove system starts at just $23 per month for security monitoring. When you add cameras to the system, it goes up to $33 per month. Of that $33, $5 per month goes towards a credit on your account that you can use to purchase more equipment.

So for about $28 per month out of pocket, you get a really nice security system with cameras.

## Why SimpliSafe And Ring Are Only “Honorable Mentions”

You’ll see the SimpliSafe and Ring systems on a lot of the “ADT alternative” lists online. The reason that I’m not including them in this article is that they don’t have two-way talk through the panel.

The other reason is that the cameras on both these systems do not record if you don’t have Wi-Fi.

That being said, both of these systems have some unique features. The SimpliSafe system has a really nice feature called “Active Guard”, where a representative at the monitoring station will monitor your outdoor cameras on your behalf, and if they see someone doing something sketchy, they can talk to them through the camera, and even dispatch to the police.

The Ring system is probably the most affordable security system on the market, and they make some great cameras.

You’ll also see Frontpoint on a lot of lists as an ADT alternative. I haven’t had the Frontpoint system in my house, so I’m not going to rank it in this article.

## Should You Switch To ADT’s DIY Line Instead?

If you’re an existing ADT customer and the ONLY reason you want to leave is because of the contract, you may want to consider switching to the DIY version of ADT.

You purchase all the equipment up front (I paid $350 for my system) and then the monitoring is month-to-month.

## What Happens To Your Equipment When You Switch Providers?

If you’re an existing ADT customer, I’d Highly recommend reading the terms of your contract to see what you’re getting yourself into.

Some contracts state that you own the equipment after X number of years, and some contracts have you paying for the equipment over the life of the contract, and when you’re done, you have to give it back to them.

If you have Nest cameras, you can still use them after you cancel ADT (you just use the Google Home app).

If you have an existing wireless or hardwired security system in your home, both Vivint and ADT can take over those sensors (as long as they’re in good working order).

## Which Security System Should You Buy?

If you want all the features that ADT offers, plus a few more, and you DON’T want to be tied to a contract, get Vivint.

If you want to save a few dollars per month and you don’t care about the “smart home” stuff, get the Cove system.

If you already have ADT, and you have a LOT of cameras (5 or more) and just want to be able to control a couple smart devices around the house, then just stick with ADT.
