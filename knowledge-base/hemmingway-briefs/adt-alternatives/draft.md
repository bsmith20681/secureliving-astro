

If you’re an existing ADT customer looking to switch providers or you’re on the fence about buying an ADT system in the first place, this article is for you. I’ve had ADT in my home as well as the other major home security companies (Vivint, SimpliSafe, Ring and Cove) so I can give you first-hand experience with each. Below are my recommendations for the best ADT alternatives and why I recommend them.

## My Top Picks

*If you want all the features ADT has to offer, get Vivint*
*If you want a more affordable system, get Cove*
*Don’t get SimpliSafe or Ring if you currently have ADT*

## What To Look For In A Replacement

There are a few key features of the ADT system that you’ll want in a replacement:

1. Cellular Monitoring – Don’t get a system that compromises on the monitoring service. Make sure it’s monitored through a cellular connection.
2. Two-Way Voice Through The Keypad – This is a really good feature that ADT has. If a break-in occurs and you’re in the house, you won’t be able to answer your phone. The rep at the monitoring station can speak to you directly through the alarm panel.
3. Remote Access To Cameras – This seems like a no brainer but there are a couple systems on the market that don’t offer this.
4. Smart Home Features – The ability to control lights, thermostats, door locks in the ADT app is a nice feature. You’ll want a replacement system to offer this as well.

## Vivint: The Closest Match

Vivint is ADT’s closest competitor and it has everything that ADT does, plus a little more.

### Monitoring and Two-Way Voice

The system is monitored 24-7 to police, fire and medical. It has a touchscreen keypad and it’s monitored through a cellular connection (just like ADT). If your Wi-Fi or power goes out, the system will still work. It also comes with two-way talk through the keypad. If the alarm is triggered, the rep at the monitoring station can speak to you directly through the panel.

### Cameras and Deter Mode

The cameras have all the core features the ADT (Nest) cameras have (HD video, night vision, two-way talk etc) but they have a few additional ones. Probably the most unique feature is what they call “Deter Mode”. When someone walks in front of the camera, it will play a whistling sound at them, the LED light around the lens will start to flash and if you get the floodlight attachment (for the outdoor camera), it will light up your entire yard. This is a great way to deter a break in.

The ADT (Nest) cameras will continue to record without Wi-Fi but only up to one hour. The Vivint cameras will record indefinitely without Wi-Fi.

### Smart Home Automation

The system can connect to any Z-Wave device in your house (like ADT). Where Vivint excels over ADT is the design of the app and setting up smart home automations. It’s so much more user friendly and you can get more granular with each device.

### Installation, Pricing and Contract

Like the ADT system, it is professionally installed. When I recorded my video, Vivint was running a promotion for a free doorbell camera and free installation.

If you purchase the equipment upfront, the service is month-to-month (no contract). You can also finance the equipment (0% interest) for 42 or 60 months.

## Cove: The Budget Pick

The Cove system is a little more affordable than ADT (and Vivint) but it doesn’t skimp on any of the core home security features.

### Monitoring, Panel and Cameras

The Cove system has a touchscreen panel (similar to ADT and Vivint). It comes with two-way voice built into the panel (so if the alarm goes off and you can’t answer your phone, the agent at the monitoring station can speak to you through the panel). The system is monitored 24-7 to police, fire, medical. It connects to the monitoring station through your Wi-Fi and it has a cellular backup (whereas the ADT system just uses a cellular connection).

The cameras have all the core features (HD video, night vision, motion detection, two-way talk) and they record without Wi-Fi (there is a built in SD card in each camera). Unlike the ADT or Vivint system, there is no option to get 24/7 continuous recording. The Cove cameras will only record in clips.

### Installation and Pricing

The Cove system is a DIY system (unlike ADT which is professionally installed). When I recorded my video, they were running a promotion for 70% off equipment and a free indoor camera. I paid about $250 up front for the equipment and $33 per month to monitor the system (with access to the cameras). Out of that $33 per month, $5 is put towards a credit on your account that you can use to purchase additional equipment. So your “real” monthly cost is about $28.

## Why SimpliSafe and Ring Are Only Honorable Mentions

There are two main features that SimpliSafe and Ring don’t have that ADT does that kept them off the list:

1. No two-way voice through the alarm keypad
2. Cameras won’t record without Wi-Fi

If you do go with SimpliSafe, I highly recommend their new “Active Guard” service where a rep at the monitoring station will actually monitor your outdoor cameras on your behalf. Ring is probably the lowest priced system on the market (and their cameras are great) but if you already have ADT, it’s not worth switching.

You may notice that Frontpoint is on a lot of the “best ADT alternative” lists on Google. The reason you don’t see it here is because I haven’t used that system.

## Should You Switch To The DIY Line Instead?

If the only issue you have with your ADT system is the contract, it may be worth considering their DIY line. I paid $350 for my ADT DIY system and the service is month-to-month (no contract).

## What Happens To Your Equipment When You Switch Security Companies?

If you’re an existing ADT customer, look at the terms of your contract before you switch. You may be on the hook for a penalty if you cancel early.

The Nest cameras that come with the ADT system are non-proprietary, meaning you can continue to use them after you cancel.

Vivint is the only one of the ADT alternatives that can take over existing equipment (hardwired or wireless).

## Which One Should You Buy?

If you want all the same features as ADT (plus a couple extra), I recommend Vivint. If you want a lower price point and don’t care about smart home capabilities, get Cove. If you like the ADT brand and the only problem you have is the length of the contract, consider the DIY line.
