# ADT Security System Alternatives

* * *

SimpliSafe is affordable and contract-free, Vivint offers advanced automation, and Cove is a budget-friendly DIY option. All are viable ADT alternatives.

If you’re looking for reliable home security, an ADT system will get you there. But not everyone is a fan of ADT. Compared to other home security systems, ADT’s professionally installed plans are steep on price and restrictive on contracts, though its DIY platform, ADT Blu, is a contract-free exception.

Luckily, there are some solid alternatives out there if ADT isn’t the system for you. Check out our picks: SimpliSafe, Cove, and Vivint. All let you arm and disarm your system remotely, with great customer service and few false alarms. Let’s get started.

    The story of SimpliSafe, a wireless DIY alarm system, began in 2006 after a team of Harvard Business School students noticed an uptick in burglaries.1 They wanted to create a security system for first-time buyers and apartment dwellers.
    
    Today, the company offers stick-on security equipment, no-fuss setup, and flexible month-to-month monitoring plans. SimpliSafe’s security cameras are battery-powered for easy installations. Even still, they both recorded clear, smooth video footage in our tests.
    
    SimpliSafe is poised as a great ADT alternative especially to renters. Since it doesn’t require a monitoring contract, it’s easy to get out of paying [monthly fees](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/best/no-monthly-fee/) when the budget is tight. There are no penalties or extra fees for canceling either, unlike with ADT Pro Install and Vivint.
    
    [SimpliSafe’s monitoring rates](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/simplisafe/) are also cheaper than ADT Pro Install, which typically runs $49.99 per month. You can get professional monitoring for as low as $22.99 per month, a hair below the $24.99 that ADT’s DIY platform, ADT Blu, starts at.
    
    And then there’s the fact that it is a DIY system, so there are no installation fees involved. So compared to ADT Pro Install, SimpliSafe is a more practical choice overall.
    
    Are there downsides? SimpliSafe’s technology isn’t quite as advanced as ADT’s. See our [SimpliSafe Outdoor Camera review](https://www.security.org/security-cameras/simplisafe-outdoor/) or our [SimpliSafe Video Doorbell Series 2](https://www.security.org/doorbell-camera/simplisafe/review/) review for examples. There are only three camera options available, and the video doorbell requires hardwiring without a battery backup. Meanwhile, ADT offers some of the most advanced cameras in the market, especially now that it partnered with Google Nest to include Nest Cams in its packages. Nest Cams offer features like person detection and facial recognition for a truly futuristic home security.

## Final Thoughts

Every security system has its pros and cons. To recap …

-   SimpliSafe is easy to install and contract-free.
-   Vivint is loaded with intelligent motion detection features and offers top-notch equipment.
-   Cove is both a [DIY security system](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/best/diy/) and an [affordable security system](https://www.security.org/home-security-systems/best/affordable/).

If you’re looking for something different from ADT, it’s good to know there are viable options out there.
