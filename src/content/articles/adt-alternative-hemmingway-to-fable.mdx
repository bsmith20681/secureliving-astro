I've tested ADT, Vivint, SimpliSafe and Ring in my own home, and I bought and installed a Cove system too.

If you're an existing ADT customer whose contract is nearly up, or are just starting your search for a new home security system, this article is for you. I'll be covering two of the top ADT competitors and give you the reasons why.

## How I Tested These Home Security Systems

Every home security system in this review is one I've paid for and lived with. ADT was professionally installed in my house and got a full review. Vivint, SimpliSafe and Ring each got the same treatment and their own review. Cove I bought with my own money and installed myself.

On paper ADT and Vivint look identical. The differences only show up once you've used both apps side by side.

## What To Look For In An ADT Competitor

There are 4 main features you want to look for in any alternatives to ADT:

1. Professional security monitoring through a cellular connection
2. Two-way voice through the keypad
3. Remote access to security cameras (view live footage and previously recorded video from an app)
4. Smart home capabilities (control lights, thermostats, door locks, etc)

Vivint checks all 4 boxes. Cove skips the automation piece and makes up for it on price.

## Vivint: Best Overall Alternative To ADT

Vivint is a direct competitor to ADT and does everything the ADT system can do, plus a little bit more. My [Vivint review](/home-security-systems/vivint-review/) covers the whole system.

### Monitoring And Panel

<figure>
  <Image src={vivintPanelArmed} alt="Vivint touchscreen panel showing the system armed" />
  <figcaption>The Vivint touchscreen panel.</figcaption>
</figure>

Vivint is a full service home security system. Professional monitoring covers police, fire and medical 24/7. It comes with a touchscreen keypad (Vivint calls it the Smart Hub), just like ADT. It operates over a cellular connection (no need for a landline or Wi-Fi) and has two-way talk built into the panel.

If the alarm system gets tripped and someone is in the house, you're not going to be picking up your phone. An agent talking to you through the panel gets a much quicker response.

### Cameras And Deter Mode

<figure>
  <Image src={vivintDeter} alt="Vivint Outdoor Camera Pro with its deter mode light ring flashing" />
  <figcaption>Vivint's outdoor camera in deter mode.</figcaption>
</figure>

Vivint offers some features with their cameras that ADT does not. Most notable is a feature called "[deter mode](/home-security-systems/what-is-deter-mode-on-vivint/)". An ADT camera sends you an alert and records. A Vivint camera does that too, then it plays a **whistling sound** and a **light around the lens** begins to flash. You can also attach a floodlight that will illuminate your yard.

<figure>
  <Image src={vivintFloodlightNight} alt="Vivint floodlight camera lighting up a backyard at night" />
  <figcaption>The Vivint floodlight attachment at night.</figcaption>
</figure>

The Vivint cameras will also record without Wi-Fi. (ADT cameras do too, but only for a maximum of **one hour**.) Vivint saves the footage to the panel, so you can watch it there while the internet is down.

### Smart Home App And Automations

<figure>
  <Image src={vivintLockApp} alt="Locking a door from the Vivint mobile app" />
  <figcaption>Locking a smart lock from the Vivint app.</figcaption>
</figure>

The Vivint system is compatible with any Z-Wave smart home device, just like ADT. But the app is exceptionally well designed and you can get very granular with the automations you create. You can set up a rule that arms the alarm, turns off the lights and locks the doors at 9 p.m. Monday through Friday. ADT connects to the same smart locks and switches, but its app isn't as granular. I go deeper on the two apps in my [Vivint vs ADT review](/home-security-systems/vivint-vs-adt/).

### Vivint Pricing And Contract

Home security monitoring starts at **$29.99 per month** for police, fire and medical dispatch plus the app. Smart home control brings it to $39.99, and each camera adds $5 per month. ADT starts at **$42.99** and goes to $48.99 once you add cameras, so with a camera or two the monthly bills land close together.

Equipment starts around **$599**, which is typical for professionally installed security systems, and installation is included. Pay for the equipment upfront and the service is **month-to-month** with no contract. Finance it at 0% APR over 42 or 60 months and the contract runs until the balance is paid off. ADT works the same way, with a 12-month minimum and financing up to 36 months.

Before you switch, know that Vivint's cameras are proprietary and stop working if you cancel. ADT's Nest cameras keep working after you cancel.

When I recorded my video, Vivint was running a promotion for a <a href={vivintCompanyData.website}>free doorbell camera and free installation</a>.

## Cove: Most Affordable ADT Alternative

The Cove home security system is a little more affordable than ADT, without sacrificing any of the core features. My [Cove review](/home-security-systems/cove-review/) has the full rundown.

<figure>
  <Image src={covePanel} alt="Cove touchscreen panel disarmed and ready to arm" />
  <figcaption>The Cove touchscreen panel.</figcaption>
</figure>

Like ADT and Vivint, Cove has a touchscreen panel with two-way voice built in. You get **24/7 professional monitoring** to police, fire and medical. It operates over Wi-Fi with a cellular backup, so if the power or the internet goes out, you still have monitoring.

### Cameras And Their Limits

<figure>
  <Image src={coveNightCam} alt="Cove outdoor camera night vision view of a pool area" />
  <figcaption>Night footage from the Cove outdoor camera.</figcaption>
</figure>

Cove security cameras do everything you would expect: view live footage from the app, get motion alerts, talk to people through the camera, etc. The cameras (at least the outdoor one) will record without Wi-Fi thanks to a built-in SD card.

Where the Cove home security system falls short is that it only records in short video clips. ADT and Vivint record in short clips out of the box too, but both let you upgrade to 24/7 recording. Cove has no upgrade. The other gap is home automation. There's none, so if you use your ADT app to control smart locks or a smart thermostat, Cove won't replace that.

### DIY Installation And Cove Pricing

<figure>
  <Image src={coveSensorInstall} alt="Peeling the adhesive backing off a Cove door sensor" />
  <figcaption>Cove sensors peel and stick, no drilling needed.</figcaption>
</figure>

Installation is DIY, unlike the two professionally installed security systems above. The hub plugs into your router, the panel walks you through syncing each sensor, and the sensors peel and stick. The security equipment took me about **30 minutes**. The cameras were the frustrating part. Getting them to sync to the panel ate up most of the three hours I spent on the whole DIY install.

Cove starts at **$23 per month** for home security monitoring and **$33 per month** once you add cameras. When I purchased my Cove system I paid **$250 upfront** (with 70% off and a free indoor camera). **$5** of that $33 per month is a credit you can use for future equipment purchases, so the true out of pocket cost is closer to **$28 per month**. Extra cameras don't raise the bill.

There's **no contract**, so you can cancel whenever you want with no penalty. The cameras are Eufy cameras, so if you cancel, you can resync them to the Eufy app and keep using them.

## Honorable Mentions: Ring And SimpliSafe

<figure>
  <Image src={simplisafeKeypad} alt="SimpliSafe keypad showing home mode" />
  <figcaption>The SimpliSafe keypad.</figcaption>
</figure>

[Ring](/home-security-systems/ring-review/) and [SimpliSafe](/home-security-systems/simplisafe-review/) are two other popular DIY home security systems. The reason they didn't make my list of ADT alternatives is because they do not have two-way voice through the control panel. And the cameras will not record in the event of a Wi-Fi outage.

<figure>
  <Image src={ringBaseStation} alt="Ring Alarm base station glowing blue" />
  <figcaption>The Ring Alarm base station.</figcaption>
</figure>

They still have their place among DIY security systems, and neither requires a contract. Ring monitoring starts at $20 per month and makes sense if you already own Ring cameras. SimpliSafe monitoring starts at $32.99 per month and it's the only one with Active Guard, where a live agent watches your outdoor cameras and talks to a prowler through the camera. My [SimpliSafe vs ADT review](/home-security-systems/simplisafe-vs-adt/) covers that trade.

## Which Of These ADT Alternatives Should You Pick?

- Get Vivint if you want all the features ADT has to offer, plus a few extras
- Get Cove if you want a more affordable alternative to ADT and don't need smart home control
- Look at SimpliSafe if live video monitoring matters more to you than two-way voice at the panel
