## How To Cancel Vivint Service

Here are the steps to follow:

1. Review your Vivint agreement. Find out when your "initial term" ends and how much you still owe on your equipment.
2. Call Vivint customer service at **1-800-678-2635** to begin the cancellation process.
3. Ask Vivint if you still owe money on the equipment. You can find this info online before calling by logging into the dashboard and looking at your invoice.
4. Put your cancellation request in writing and send it to Vivint at least **30 days** before you want your service to be terminated.
5. Get written confirmation from Vivint that they've received your cancellation request.
6. After you cancel, monitor your account to make sure you're not being charged for service you don't have.

Your written notice should include:

- Date of letter
- Your Vivint service number
- A clear statement that you want to cancel your contract
- A brief explanation of why you are canceling
- Your signature

<Admonition>
  If you cancel Vivint, the only balance you owe is the equipment balance. If
  you have the equipment paid off, then you don't need to pay for anything
  extra. If you do have a balance on the equipment then you must pay off what is
  owed. You can't cancel the service, unless the equipment is paid off.
</Admonition>

### How To Cancel Vivint Online

The Vivint agreement gives you no way to cancel from the mobile app or from your online account. It requires you to submit **written notice** if you want to cancel. Write a short letter with the details listed above, send it where the rep tells you, and keep a copy.

## Does Vivint Have A Contract?

Only if you choose to finance your equipment. If you pay up front for your equipment your service is **month to month** and you can cancel at any time without penalty. That's what I did when I bought my system.

If you owe a balance on the equipment then you are also obligated to pay for the monitoring service. You can pay off the equipment balance early, and then discontinue your service.

### How Long Is A Vivint Contract?

If you choose to finance your equipment with Vivint you can get **0% APR** financing for **42 or 60 months**. You have to continue to have service from Vivint for as long as you have a balance on your equipment. You can't just cancel your monitoring service and continue to make payments on your equipment.

The Vivint agreement says "The initial term starts on the day this agreement is signed." After the initial term ends your service is month to month and "either party may terminate this agreement at any time after the initial term upon at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party."

## Does Vivint Have A Cancellation Fee?

There is no set cancellation fee with Vivint. If you financed your equipment you will owe whatever **remaining balance** is left. The exact amount you owe when you cancel will depend on the terms of the contract you signed. Some Vivint customers have said that they were required to pay Vivint the monthly monitoring fees for the remaining length of their contract when they cancelled. Ask Vivint for your payoff amount in writing.

### Can You Cancel Vivint Before Paying Off Equipment?

No. If you want to cancel your Vivint service before you pay off your equipment you have to pay the remaining balance. The agreement says that once you've paid everything you owe on the equipment, you can terminate **"without penalty or early termination fee"** with 30 days' prior written notice, even if you're still inside the initial term.

## How To Get Out Of A Vivint Contract Without Penalty

There are a few ways to get out of a Vivint contract without paying a penalty:

- If you are in one of the following situations call Vivint and ask them to review your contract:
  - **Death** of the account holder
  - **Bankruptcy**
  - Moving to an **assisted living** facility
  - You are in the military

Vivint's Exceptions Review Team will review your request case by case. They will ask you for documentation. Calling Vivint to request an exception does not guarantee that they will release you from your contract.

Vivint's support site also says customers **65 or older** who cancel because of a medical condition or a move to a care facility may be eligible to leave without the fee.

Vivint will waive any early termination or cancellation fees for active U.S. military personnel, or anyone who has been discharged in the last 12 months, who are:

- Being deployed for **90 days or longer**
- Required to move locations, even on the same base
- Retiring after 20 or more years of service
- Being medically discharged and are moving residences

Other veterans and their dependents are not eligible. You will need to provide current orders or other official documents, such as deployment orders or medical discharge paperwork.

There is also a deferral program for service members who are on temporary assignment away from their permanent residence for **less than 12 months**. If you qualify for this program Vivint will suspend your agreement and the term of your contract will not run while it is suspended.

A regular, non-medical discharge is not grounds to cancel your contract. But if you want to move your system or transfer your agreement to a friend or family member Vivint will waive the fee.

You can also transfer your Vivint contract to another person. You'll need to get approval from Vivint first.

## How To Cancel Vivint Within 3 Days

Vivint has what is called a "buyer's right to cancel" clause. This allows you to cancel within **three business days** of signing by giving written notice in person, by telegram, or by mail. The notice has to say that you don't want the goods or services.

  <Image

Keep in mind that the three day count begins when you sign the contract. It doesn't wait for your system to be installed.

If you cancel in this window, Vivint may not keep any part of a cash down payment. You already have the form: at signing you confirm that you received two copies of it.

The same clause has a medical exit: you can cancel if a doctor's order means you physically can't receive the services, and Vivint keeps only a pro rata share for what you used. My clause cites Florida's rules, so read the wording in yours.

## If I Cancel Vivint Will My Equipment Still Work?

If you cancel your service with Vivint here is what will happen:

You lose access to the Vivint app. You can no longer watch your cameras or download video clips from your phone. Your cameras will continue to record locally to the Vivint panel. Your alarm system will still arm and disarm and you will get a siren if there is an alarm. But the system no longer has professional monitoring, so no one will call the police, fire department or ambulance. The keypad on your door lock and thermostat will work, as will the controls on the Vivint panel, but you can no longer control them from the app and you lose any smart home "automations" that you had set up.

Section 13 of the agreement also authorizes Vivint to remotely disconnect the system software once service ends, so don't count on the panel features lasting forever.

Vivint cameras are proprietary. You can't take them and hook them up to other security systems. If you are moving houses Vivint has a program where they will move your system.
