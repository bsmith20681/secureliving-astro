You've decided to leave SimpliSafe, and the good news is that it's one of the easiest home security companies to cancel. There's **no contract** and **no cancellation fee**. You contact support, prove the account is yours, and wait for a confirmation email.

I went through the cancellation process myself and documented it all here for you. If you haven't decided what to replace it with yet, I've also compared the [best SimpliSafe alternatives](/home-security-systems/simplisafe-alternatives/) I've tested in my own house.

## How To Cancel SimpliSafe Step By Step

1. Find your SimpliSafe safe word
2. Make sure you are the "account owner" or have the owner make the request
3. Call customer service at **1-888-783-8441**, or chat with them online
4. Tell the rep that you want to end your monitoring plan
5. Wait for the email from SimpliSafe confirming your cancellation
6. Check your billing to make sure the charges have stopped

Only the owner can make the request, and the rep may ask for the safe word to verify your identity before making any change. SimpliSafe says the check is there for your safety and security, so nobody else can pick up the phone and shut off your monitoring.

Once the cancellation is processed, the confirmation email goes to the email address on file. Save it in case a charge shows up later.

## Can You Cancel SimpliSafe Online Or In The App?

You can upgrade at any time in the SimpliSafe app or in your online account.

But if you want to downgrade or cancel, you'll have to call or chat with SimpliSafe.

If you're a resident of certain cities and states you can initiate a cancellation request online. Go to simplisafe.com during standard hours of operation, click on the "Account" icon on the home screen, then "Security Dashboard", and then "Manage Account." The option is at the bottom of the screen.

## SimpliSafe Cancellation Fees And Contract

SimpliSafe's service is month-to-month. You can leave at any time, for any reason, with **no penalty**.

If you purchase your equipment directly from SimpliSafe there is a **60 day** trial period. You can return the equipment within 60 days for a full refund.

## What Still Works After Your Cancellation

SimpliSafe is one of the few home security companies out there that allows you to use their system with **no monthly fee** at all.

If you aren't paying for monitoring you can still arm and disarm your system like normal. However, if the alarm is tripped it won't notify the police department.

You also lose the cellular backup, so the free system leans entirely on your internet. I tested what happens in that case in [does SimpliSafe work without WiFi](/home-security-systems/does-simplisafe-work-without-wifi/).
