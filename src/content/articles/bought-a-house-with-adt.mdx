So you just bought a house with ADT already installed. Congrats on the new home! This is actually a pretty common situation. The previous owner left an ADT system behind, and now you're trying to figure out what to do with it.

I get asked about this a lot. Can you use the existing ADT equipment without paying for monitoring? Do the cameras still work without a subscription? Can you reactivate the old system? What about switching to a different security company?

I'll walk through all of it.

## How to Reactivate an Existing ADT System

If you want to get the ADT system up and running, you'll need to call ADT to reactivate it. Their phone number is <a href={`tel:${companyData.phoneUnformatted}`}>{companyData.phone}</a> and you can also check out their <a href={companyData.website}>website</a>. When you call, a sales rep will set up a new account under your name, go over their monitoring packages, and talk through pricing.

The ADT reactivation process is pretty straightforward. Since you already have the equipment installed in the home, you skip the setup fee and you don't have to pay upfront for new equipment. That alone can save you a few hundred dollars compared to a brand new installation.

Once you pick a package, ADT will schedule an installer to come out and activate the existing system. The installer will test all of the sensors, set up your **new pin code**, and make sure everything is working. After that, you'll be good to go.

The process is quick and painless. Most of the time they can come out same day or next day.

## Can I use ADT Without Monitoring or Paying for a Subscription?

This is the big one. A lot of people who moved into a house with an ADT system just want to know if they can use it without paying for monitoring or a subscription.

The short answer is yes, but with limited functionality. The alarm system will still work as a local alarm without professional monitoring. 

This means you can arm and disarm the system like normal and the siren will still sound if the alarm is tripped. However, nobody from the monitoring station will contact you or your local police department. 

Here's a more detailed list on what will work without paying for the service:

- The siren still sounds when the alarm is triggered
- Door, window, and motion sensors still detect activity
- Smoke detectors and CO detectors still sound locally
- You can arm and disarm the system with the pin code
- Smart home devices will still function, just not from the app.

Here's what you lose without a subscription:

- No access to the ADT app on your phone
- ADT won't dispatch police, fire, or medical services
- No remote arm/disarm from your phone
- No camera access (more on this below)

If someone breaks in while your ADT alarm is armed, the siren will still go off. And honestly, most burglars aren't going to stick around when they hear that. The siren alone is a solid deterrent. You just won't have the monitoring center calling the police on your behalf.

### Getting the Pin Code From the Previous Owner

If the previous owner left you the pin code, you're all set. Just use it to arm and disarm the system as needed.

If you didn't get the pin code, your best bet is reaching out to the previous homeowner. This is one of the most common issues people run into when they move into a house with an existing ADT security system, because if you call ADT to ask for the PIN code they will not give it out unless you are a new customer.
That's a security policy they have in place and honestly it makes sense.

If you can't get a hold of the previous owner, here are some commonly used pin codes you can try:

- 1234
- 2468
- 7923

None of these are guaranteed to work, but they're worth trying.

### Does the ADT App Work Without a Subscription?

No. The ADT app does not work without an active subscription. This is one of the most common misconceptions I see. People assume that because the physical equipment still functions, the app should too. But ADT requires a paid monitoring plan to access the app.

Without a subscription, you can't remotely arm or disarm the system,receive push notifications, or control smart home devices. All of that requires the monitoring service.

## Can You Use ADT Cameras Without Service?

  <Image src={adtDoorbellCamera} alt="ADT doorbell camera installed" />

    ADT cameras like this Nest doorbell require an active subscription to function
  </figcaption>

If the previous cameras are from Nest, then you can use those cameras without ADT service. You'll need to use the Google Home app to connect the cameras and you can watch them from there. 

There there are older ADT branded cameras then unfortunately those will not work on their own and you will need to activate the service in order to use them. 

This applies to all ADT cameras: indoor, outdoor, and doorbell cameras. Even if the previous owner left cameras installed throughout the home, they won't do anything unless you sign up for the $48.99/month ADT camera plan.

## What Happens to ADT Equipment After Cancellation?

<figure>
  <Image src={adtDoorSensor} alt="ADT door sensor installed on a door frame" />
  <figcaption>
    Wired sensors like door contacts can usually transfer to another security company
  </figcaption>
</figure>

If the previous owner cancelled their ADT service before selling the house, all the physical equipment stays. ADT doesn't come repossess the sensors, panel, or wiring when someone cancels. It's all still there and it still belongs to whoever owns the home.

After cancellation, the system works exactly like I described above in the "without monitoring" section. The alarm, sensors, and smoke detectors function locally. Cameras go dark. The app stops working. But the hardware is still physically installed and the basic alarm functions still operate.

The equipment doesn't have an expiration date or anything like that. I've seen homes with ADT systems that are 10+ years old and the sensors still work fine. The batteries in the sensors will need replacing eventually, but the equipment itself holds up.

If you're using ADT equipment after cancellation and want full functionality again, you can call ADT customer service at <a href={`tel:${companyData.phoneUnformatted}`}>{companyData.phone}</a> to reactivate the system under a new account.

## Can You Use ADT Equipment With Another Security Company?

Most other companies can use existing sensors and smart home equipment, but they cannot use the panel or cameras. 

Other larger companies such as Vivint can typically use ADT equipment, but if you purcahse a DIY system such as SimpliSafe or Cove then neither of those systems will be able to use any of the ADT equipment.

## I Accidentally Triggered the ADT Alarm. How Do I Turn It Off?

If you set the ADT alarm off by accident, punch in the pin code on the panel and it should stop.

If you don't have the pin code, unplug the ADT panel from the wall and then remove the backup battery. Just unplugging it won't do it since the backup battery will keep the system running for another 3-4 hours. Here are some instructions on how to [replace the battery](https://www.adt.com/help/faq/security-services-features/replace-system-battery). Those same steps will show you how to remove it.
