Maybe you already have ADT and you're done with it. Or you're about to sign up and want to see what else is out there. I've had ADT installed in my own home, along with Vivint, Cove, SimpliSafe, and Ring, so I can tell you which alternatives to ADT hold up and which home security companies cut corners.

## What To Look For In A Home Security System Like ADT

ADT sets a high bar on security. If you switch, I wouldn't give up any of these:

1. Cellular monitoring, so the alarm system keeps reaching the monitoring center when your power or Wi-Fi goes out.
2. Two-way voice through the keypad. If someone's in the house, you probably can't grab your phone, and an agent who can talk to you through the panel gets help moving faster.
3. Remote access to your security cameras, with live view and recorded clips on your phone.
4. Smart home control of Z-Wave locks, lights, and thermostats, if you use that with ADT now.

Every home security system below has entry sensors, motion sensors, and 24/7 professional monitoring for police, fire, and medical safety. The differences show up in the four things above.

## 1. Vivint: The Closest ADT Competitor

Vivint goes head to head with ADT, and its security system does everything ADT's does with a few extras on top. If you like ADT's features and want something better, this is where I'd go.

<figure>
  <Image src={vivintPanelArmed} alt="Vivint touchscreen panel showing the system armed" />
  <figcaption>The Vivint panel has the same two-way voice you get with ADT.</figcaption>
</figure>

### Same Monitoring And Two-Way Voice

You get a touchscreen keypad, 24/7 monitoring, and a cellular connection, so you don't need a landline and the system keeps running if your Wi-Fi drops. Two-way talk is built into the panel just like ADT's. On home security, you lose nothing.

### Cameras With Deter Mode

The security cameras are where Vivint pulls ahead. When someone walks up to an ADT camera, you get an alert and it records. Vivint does that too, but it also whistles at the person, flashes an LED ring around the lens, and with the floodlight attachment, lights up the whole yard. That's [Deter Mode](/home-security-systems/what-is-deter-mode-on-vivint/). If you're somewhere you shouldn't be and a camera starts whistling at you, you're not sticking around.

<figure>
  <Image src={vivintFloodlightNight} alt="Vivint outdoor camera floodlight lighting up a backyard at night" />
  <figcaption>The floodlight attachment lights up the backyard when Deter Mode kicks in.</figcaption>
</figure>

Video quality is excellent, and the cameras keep recording to the panel when the Wi-Fi goes out. ADT's Nest cameras only cover about an hour without Wi-Fi. More on that in my guide on [whether Vivint works without Wi-Fi](/home-security-systems/does-vivint-work-without-wifi/).

### Better Smart Home Automation

Both security systems connect to anything Z-Wave, so on paper they look the same. The difference is the smart home app. Say you want the panel to arm, the lights to shut off, and the doors to lock every weeknight at 9 PM. You can build that in either app, but Vivint's flow is better designed and gets more granular with each device. You don't notice it until you use both apps side by side, which I did in my [Vivint vs ADT review](/home-security-systems/vivint-vs-adt/).

</figure>

### Vivint Pricing And Contract

Monitoring runs **$29.99 to $49.99 a month**, and equipment starts around **$599**. Pay upfront and there's no contract. Finance the equipment and the contract can run up to 60 months, which is longer than ADT's. Professional installation is included.

## 2. Cove: Same Core Security For Less

Cove is the alternative to ADT I'd point most budget shoppers toward. It costs a lot less than ADT or Vivint and keeps the home security features that matter.

<figure>
  <Image src={covePanel} alt="Cove touchscreen panel disarmed and ready to arm" />
  <figcaption>Cove's touchscreen panel, with two-way voice built in.</figcaption>
</figure>

### Touchscreen Panel And Cellular Backup

You get a touchscreen keypad with two-way voice, so the monitoring station can talk to you through the panel the same way ADT's can. Monitoring runs over Wi-Fi with a cellular backup, so you're still covered if the power or internet goes out. Installation is DIY, and the sensors stick on with adhesive.

<figure>
  <Image src={coveSensor} alt="Installing a Cove door sensor above a door frame" />
  <figcaption>Cove's door sensors stick on with adhesive. No drilling.</figcaption>
</figure>

### Cameras That Record Without Wi-Fi

The cameras give you alerts, two-way talk, and recorded clips in the app. Each one has a built-in SD card, so the cameras keep recording when your Wi-Fi goes down. ADT and Vivint offer that too. SimpliSafe and Ring don't.

<figure>
  <Image src={coveNightCam} alt="Cove outdoor security camera night view of a pool area" />
  <figcaption>Night footage from my Cove outdoor camera.</figcaption>
</figure>

### What Cove Is Missing

Two things bug me about Cove:

- The cameras record clips only. ADT and Vivint also record clips out of the box, but both let you upgrade to 24/7 continuous recording. Cove has no upgrade.
- There's no smart home automation, so no locks, lights, or thermostat control.

If you don't use either one today, you won't miss them.

### Cove Pricing

Price is the big reason to pick Cove. Monitoring is **$23 a month** for security only and **$33 a month** with cameras, with **no contract**. I paid about **$250 upfront** for my equipment during a 70% off sale. Of that $33, $5 goes back to your account as a credit for more equipment, so my real cost is closer to **$28 a month**.

## Honorable Mentions: SimpliSafe And Ring

SimpliSafe and Ring are both popular DIY security systems, and I've tested both in my home. I left them off the main list for two reasons:

- Neither has two-way voice through the panel, so the monitoring station can't talk to you through the keypad during an alarm.
- Their cameras don't record without Wi-Fi. When the internet drops, so does your footage.

## Switching Security Systems: Contracts And Equipment

Before you cancel, check your ADT contract. Breaking it early usually means a fee, so a lot of people wait until it runs out. Your Nest cameras keep working for free through the Google Home app, but ADT's sensors won't work with Cove, SimpliSafe, or Ring. Vivint can often take over existing hardwired sensors, which saves money on equipment.
