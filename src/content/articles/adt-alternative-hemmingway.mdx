If you're an existing ADT customer looking to change providers or are just starting your search for a new home security system, this article is for you. I'll be covering two of the top ADT alternatives and give you the reasons why.

I've tested both ADT and Vivint in my own home.

## What To Look For In An ADT Competitor

There are 4 main features you want to look for in any alternatives to ADT:

1. Professional security monitoring through a cellular connection
2. Two-way voice through the keypad
3. Remote access to security cameras (view live footage and previously recorded video from an app)
4. Smart home capabilities (control lights, thermostats, door locks, etc)

## Vivint: Best Overall Alternative To ADT

[Vivint](/home-security-systems/vivint-review/) is a direct competitor to ADT and does everything the ADT system can do, plus a little bit more.

### Monitoring And Panel

<figure>
  <Image src={vivintPanelArmed} alt="Vivint touchscreen panel showing the system armed" />
  <figcaption>The Vivint touchscreen panel.</figcaption>
</figure>

Vivint is a full service home security system. It is monitored 24/7 to police, fire and medical. It comes with a touchscreen keypad, just like ADT. It operates over a cellular connection (no need for a landline or Wi-Fi) and has two-way talk built into the panel.

### Cameras And Deter Mode

<figure>
  <Image src={vivintDeter} alt="Vivint Outdoor Camera Pro with its deter mode light ring flashing" />
  <figcaption>Vivint's outdoor camera in deter mode.</figcaption>
</figure>

Vivint offers some features with their cameras that ADT does not. Most notably is a feature called "[deter mode](/home-security-systems/what-is-deter-mode-on-vivint/)". When someone walks in front of a Vivint camera, it plays a **whistling sound** and a **light around the lens** begins to flash. You can also attach a floodlight that will illuminate your yard.

<figure>
  <Image src={vivintFloodlightNight} alt="Vivint floodlight camera lighting up a backyard at night" />
  <figcaption>The Vivint floodlight attachment at night.</figcaption>
</figure>

The Vivint cameras will also record without Wi-Fi. (ADT cameras will also record without Wi-Fi, but only for a maximum of **one hour**).

### Smart Home App And Automations

<figure>
  <Image src={vivintLockApp} alt="Locking a door from the Vivint mobile app" />
  <figcaption>Locking a door from the Vivint app.</figcaption>
</figure>

The Vivint system is compatible with any Z-Wave smart home device, just like ADT. But the app is exceptionally well designed and you can get very granular with the automations you create.

When I recorded my video, Vivint was running a promotion for a <a href={vivintCompanyData.website}>free doorbell camera and free installation</a>.

## Cove: Most Affordable ADT Alternative

The [Cove](/home-security-systems/cove-review/) home security system is a little more affordable than ADT, without sacrificing any of the core features.

<figure>
  <Image src={covePanel} alt="Cove touchscreen panel disarmed and ready to arm" />
  <figcaption>The Cove touchscreen panel.</figcaption>
</figure>

Like ADT and Vivint, Cove has a touchscreen panel with two-way voice built in. You get **24/7 professional monitoring** to police, fire and medical. It operates over Wi-Fi with a cellular backup.

### Cameras And Their Limits

<figure>
  <Image src={coveNightCam} alt="Cove outdoor camera night vision view of a pool area" />
  <figcaption>Night footage from the Cove outdoor camera.</figcaption>
</figure>

Cove security cameras do everything you would expect: view live footage from the app, get motion alerts, talk to people through the camera, etc. The cameras (at least the outdoor one) will record without Wi-Fi.

Where the Cove system falls short is that it only records in short video clips. ADT and Vivint only record in short clips as well, but you do have the option to upgrade to 24/7 recording.

When I purchased my Cove system I paid **$250 upfront** (with 70% off and a free indoor camera) and I pay **$33 per month** for monitoring and camera access. **$5** of that $33 per month is a credit you can use for future equipment purchases.

## Honorable Mentions: Ring And SimpliSafe

<figure>
  <Image src={simplisafeKeypad} alt="SimpliSafe keypad showing home mode" />

[Ring](/home-security-systems/ring-review/) and [SimpliSafe](/home-security-systems/simplisafe-review/) are two other popular DIY home security systems. The reason they didn't make my list of ADT alternatives is because they do not have two-way voice through the control panel. And the cameras will not record in the event of a Wi-Fi outage.
