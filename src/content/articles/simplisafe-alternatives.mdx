If you're looking for a new home security system to replace your current SimpliSafe system, then you've come to the right place. I have personally tested SimpliSafe, Cove, Ring, Vivint and ADT in my own house, and can tell you that there are some great alternatives to choose from.

The short answer is that for most people, **Cove** is the best alternative. It has lower equipment cost, more features, but it's the same monthly price. I go into all the details below.

However, if you already have Ring cameras then **Ring** is a great option, and if you want to upgrade to professional installation and a more in-depth smart home system then **Vivint** is the way to go.

## What To Look For In A SimpliSafe Alternative

When choosing an alternative to SimpliSafe there were three things I considered:

1.  Affordable Pricing (both monthly and equipment cost)
2.  No Contract
3.  Solid Core Security Features (mobile app, cameras, etc.)

You already have all three with SimpliSafe, so I looked for home security systems that give you more for the same money. In my testing that came down to a few things where SimpliSafe **misses the mark**:

- A touchscreen keypad in place of push buttons
- Two-way voice, so the monitoring station can talk to you through the keypad
- Cameras that keep recording when the Wi-Fi is down
- Cameras you can keep using if you ever cancel
- Home automation beyond a single door lock

When you compare that to Cove, where you get all of those features for less. You'll see pretty quickly why I think it's the better alternative.

## Why Cove Is The Best Competitor To SimpliSafe

Cove offers the same feature set as SimpliSafe at a lower price.

If you like how SimpliSafe works and you just want more for your money, this is the easiest switch to make. It's still DIY home security, still **month to month**, and still has 24/7 professional monitoring to police, fire, and medical. The keypad and the cameras are where you notice the upgrade.

### The Touchscreen Keypad And Two-Way Voice

Where Cove is different is the type of keypad you get. SimpliSafe and Ring offer a traditional push button style keypad, where as Cove gives you a full color touchscreen that I find much easier to use.

Cove's keypad also has two-way voice built in. This allows the monitoring station to talk directly to you if there is an alarm, and can be a huge benefit if you are home during a break-in.

If someone breaks in while you're inside, you're probably not in a position to answer your phone. With SimpliSafe, a phone call is how they reach you. With Cove, the rep can talk through the keypad and pick up on what's happening in the house a lot faster, and that could mean a quicker response time.

### Professional Monitoring With Cellular Backup

Just like SimpliSafe, Cove's professional monitoring connects to your home Wi-Fi network but also has a cellular backup connection in case your Wi-Fi or power goes out.

So the professional monitoring is the same as what you have now. The base station has a backup battery, and the monitoring center dispatches police, fire, or medical around the clock. You can also arm and disarm the security system from your phone and watch the cameras there.

Cove's app isn't quite as polished as some of the others I've used, but the alerts, live video, and recordings are all in one place.

### Cameras That Record Without Wi-Fi

Cove uses Eufy branded cameras that have an SD card slot built right in to the camera. This means that even if your Wi-Fi goes down, your cameras continue to record. You can also keep using the cameras if you decide to cancel your service with Cove. And you can add as many cameras as you want without it costing you extra each month.

Like SimpliSafe, Cove's cameras only record clips. Neither one has 24/7 continuous recording.

### DIY Installation

Installation of Cove is a breeze. It took me about 3 hours to install my system. You just stick the door and window sensors on with the adhesive strips, no tools required. The only part of the process I found frustrating was connecting the cameras to the system.

### Cove Pricing And Plans

Cove monitoring plans start at **$23 per month**. When you add cameras, the price goes up to **$33 per month**, the same as SimpliSafe. But here's the catch, $5 of that $33 accumulates as a credit on your account that you can use to purchase more equipment. So your true monthly cost for Cove is about **$28**.

When I recorded my video review of Cove, they were running a [70% off sale with a free indoor camera](https://www.covetrack.com/2714DHQ/9ZBW6W/). I paid about **$250** for my system after the discount, and that included 3 cameras!

I paid $519 for my SimpliSafe equipment, and that was with 50% off. So the upfront price for Cove was about _half_. The $33 plan also includes a lifetime warranty on the equipment and voice control through Alexa or Google Assistant.

## Ring Is The Pick If You Already Own Ring Cameras

A lot of people already have a Ring doorbell or a couple of Ring cameras on the house. If that's you, adding Ring's security system keeps everything in one ecosystem. Like SimpliSafe, it's DIY with no contract, so you can cancel whenever you want with no penalty.

### Monitoring And The Keypad

Ring's professional monitoring also uses your Wi-Fi network with a cellular backup connection. You get a basic push button style keypad which is okay, but I prefer the touchscreen that you get with Cove.

In other words, the core security side of Ring is about what you have now. There's no two-way voice through the keypad either, so if those two things are what bother you about SimpliSafe, Ring won't fix them. Where Ring pulls ahead is the cameras and the home automation.

### Cameras And 24/7 Recording

Ring cameras are excellent, they are easy to install and have great video quality. What I like most is that you can pay $3 a month per camera for 24/7 recording, so you aren't limited to clips when motion is detected.

That's a big plus over the SimpliSafe and Cove systems, which only record clips. A few other things I liked about the cameras:

- Video storage goes up to 180 days
- The notifications use AI to describe what the camera saw
- The Ring app is smooth and easy to use

The downside is the same one SimpliSafe has. Ring cameras depend completely on Wi-Fi, so if your internet goes out they stop recording.

### Smart Home Devices And Alexa

Ring also supports some smart home devices such as door locks and thermostats. SimpliSafe only has its own door lock, and Cove can't do any of this.

Ring is an Amazon company, so it works with Alexa and you can arm and disarm with your voice. I'd keep your expectations in check on the rest. I spent a fair amount of time trying to connect a Z-Wave door lock to my Ring system and couldn't get it to work, and it isn't compatible with the Nest thermostat. So it has more smart home capabilities than SimpliSafe, but it's still limited.

### Ring Pricing

Ring's equipment is more expensive than Cove, but if you already own Ring cameras and want to add an alarm system to your home this is a great option. Monitoring plans start at just $20 a month.

The package I bought normally sells for $500, and I got it on sale for $376. On the monthly side, the plans run all the way up to $99 if you want agents at the monitoring station watching your cameras.

## Vivint Is The Upgrade If You Want Professional Installation + Smart Home

If you buy all of Vivint's equipment upfront with no financing, then you can have a month to month agreement like you can with SimpliSafe.

But the reason I didn't include it in the list above is because it costs more. Both the equipment and monthly service cost more than the other brands. If that's not a concern for you then by all means check out Vivint, it is an excellent system.

- Smart door locks

- Garage doors
- Lights

Pricing starts at **$29.99 a month** for basic monitoring and goes to $39 with the automation features, plus $5 for each camera. Equipment starts at $599.

### Where Does ADT Fit In?

ADT is the other professionally installed system I have reviewed. But it has a minimum 12 month agreement so doesn't meet my criteria of being month to month.

It's a strong home security system otherwise. ADT uses Google Nest cameras, has a touchscreen panel, and runs **$42.99 a month**, or $48.99 with cameras. If a one-year commitment doesn't bother you and you want professional installation, it belongs on your list next to Vivint.

## If Customer Support Is Why You're Leaving

I can only speak to my own experience, and I rated SimpliSafe's customer service the same as Cove's and a little higher than Ring's. So I can't promise that switching fixes a support problem.

What I can tell you is how Cove's support treated me. Cove's customer support is open 24/7 by phone and webchat, and when I had a camera that wouldn't sync, I got a real person who walked me through the fix. If you'd like to stop being your own tech support altogether, that's where the professional installation from Vivint or ADT comes in.

## When Is SimpliSafe Worth Keeping?

There is one feature that SimpliSafe is known for, and that is live video monitoring. Their "Active Guard" service means that a live agent is watching your cameras and can alert the police if they see someone breaking in to your house.

The Cove and Vivint systems don't have anything like it. Ring does on its $99 plan, but SimpliSafe charges $49 a month for overnight coverage and $79 for 24/7. If Active Guard is the reason you bought SimpliSafe, I'd stay where you are.

## Which One Should You Choose?

As you can see there are several great alternatives to SimpliSafe.

If you want a similar system at a lower price with better equipment, go with Cove.

If you already have Ring cameras and want to add an alarm system to your home, Ring is a great choice.

And if you want to upgrade to professional installation and a much more in depth smart home system, then Vivint is worth the extra cost.

Here's the same verdict by what's bothering you about SimpliSafe:

- You want more features for the same monthly bill: <a href={coveCompanyData.website}>Cove</a>, at **$33 with cameras** and about half the upfront price.
- You don't like the keypad or the cameras: Cove again, for the touchscreen and the Eufy cameras.
- You want 24/7 recording or you already own Ring cameras: <a href={ringCompanyData.website}>Ring</a>, starting at **$20 a month**.
- You want someone else to install it and you want real home automation: <a href={vivintCompanyData.website}>Vivint</a>.

Out of all these home security alternatives, I lean towards Cove. It's affordable upfront, it costs about the same every month, and it's even a little less once you count the $5 credit.
