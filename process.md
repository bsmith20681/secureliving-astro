1. Create content cluster in keyword tool, one main topic at least 2 - 5 supporting topics
2. Go to page optimizer pro, create new page, select POP Custom
3. Grab 3 URLs for markdown reference, upload transcript, and page optimizer pro pdf
4. Have AI write article
5. Review article and edit accordingly
6. publish article and pass through page optimizer pro
7. Repeat for supporting articles (note: some supporting articles won't need a POP pdf since there are no relevant search results)
8. Once pillar and supporting topics are published, have ai add internal links.

Note: Best to take each article through the full process before moving to the next.
